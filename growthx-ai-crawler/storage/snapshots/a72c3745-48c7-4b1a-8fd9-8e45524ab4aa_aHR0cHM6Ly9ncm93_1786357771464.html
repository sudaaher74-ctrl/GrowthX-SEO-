Step 1

Problem Statement

Companies in the Gaming space (Studios or Publishers) constantly do market research & user research to:

  1. Support new / upcoming game concepts
  2. Support released titles with player retention & churn management
  3. Support released / catalogue titles with player acquisition strategies including promotions
  4. Identify games / concepts which have high retention metrics and release features inspired from them in their own games
  5. Identify games / concepts which have good traction to invest in them or publish them on a larger scale.


This research is very difficult as these companies have their own data and some data from companies like NPD, Newzoo, Mscience etc. but that isnt enough because:

  1. That data is based on a very small sample hence metrics are not close to actuals (specially engagement metrics)
  2. Monetization metrics are reliable but they are generally not breakable by regions , customer segments & cohorts etc.
  3. Marrying monetization data with other sources of data like reviews, engagement etc. is not possible as it's pre-aggregated
  4. This data is reported on a daily / weekly / monthly level
  5. This doesnt help them understand the impact of external events like competitors releases on their titles.


Solution

Yugen.ai developed a market intelligence platform tailored to their requirements, which enabled them to perform real-time analytics and has event-based analysis capabilities to gain deeper insights into their game's performance and player behaviour.

We gather data from various sources like Steam & Xbox to give these companies distilled information of:

  1. What other games are their players playing & how much time are they spending on them?
  2. What are the new releases or announcements from your competitors and how are they going to impact you?
    1. How were your competitors sales impacted by their new releases to enable you to carry out your own impact analysis
  4. Who are the users who are at a risk of churning?
  5. What are the hot and trending games which are getting very good traction?
  6. What are players saying about you on platforms like Steam & Xbox?

Leaderboard

This view enables the user to find games which are trending & leading the charts for various KPIs. This can not only be used to find games which are popular in terms of DAUs but also those which are smaller in scale but have really good retention metrics.


Performance Insights (Trends View)

This view enables the user to view historical trends of different KPIs for a particular game. This also enables them to view any metadata related to a game like their genres, key features, languages supported, system requirements, release dates etc. The trend charts in the dashboard enable the user to see patterns in Active Users, Retention Metrics, Concurrent Users, Review Scores etc. by user segments & at a Daily / Weekly / Monthly level.

Price Comparison View

This view enables the user to compare prices of different games or SKUs over time. This is important to understand not only the price gap between various SKUs but also to track when various promotions were rolled out, so that their impact on engagement can be teased out.

​

Game Comparison View

This view enables a user to choose and compare some KPIs between 2 or more games.

​


Event Comparison View

This view enables the user to select various events / announcements & releases by gaming companies and analyze their impact on a particular game's KPIs. The event tracking here has an inbuilt intelligence layer where events like metadata changes, new language support etc. as well which are not released by press are also tracked and added over time.

​

Game Migration Insights

This view enables the user to understand where their players are coming from and where are they going to. This helps them create better acquisition strategies by targeting more relevant players while this also helps them build reactivation strategies by planning to introduce features & game-modes which mimick the other games that players were interested in.

This view also has an extended version where we show other metrics like how much time are various customer segments spending on you vs your competitors etc.

Are you finding it difficult to acquire new customers or enter a new market?

  1. Since the number of Large Game Publishers & Studios (having Rev > $100M) is limited, I was expecting to be able to reach and interact with most of them within an year but it is taking much longer than expected.
  2. I have been relying on outreach campaigns (emails & Linkedin) for growth but A lot of them are inactive on Linkedin.
  3. A huge chunk of gaming companies are based out of Japan & South Korea, where english is a language barrier.
  4. It is also very difficult to connect with people given so much restructuring and consolidation going on in the industry, which means that the timing of the deal is off and even though they see value in it, they'll have to wait for some other sponsor to come in and approve it.


Are you looking to drive power usage?

  1. Absolutely! I believe that smaller companies will use insights from platforms like ours more intently to roll out experiments which in turn will make the platform impactful and give us more opportunities
    1. For example, One of our customers is a Barcelona based Game Publisher, which started using it to do market research to build their own games but they also saw that they could use our platform to identify smaller games which have amazing traction and they could approach those indie developers for publishing opportunities. This then unlocked a new area that we could work towards in the future.
    2. A lot of our work with Bungie Studios (who was our design partner), was to enable outcomes by enabling their product teams to create new roadmaps for cosmetics, releases etc. while optimizing for the impact on churn rates.


Do you have customers making requests for an integration?

We have 2 customers in the POC stage requesting integration of new data sources like E-sports data, streaming data & mobile data.


Results
