Understand your Product

(Before you begin, you need to know what your product is, what are its features, what is the problem being solved by your product?)

​

Problem:

It is estimated that 30% of all Bitcoins worth roughly $350 Billion have been lost forever due to improper management of seedphrases.

​

Every crypto wallet has only two access points:

1) Via Credentials of the wallet

2) Via Recovery method
Every crypto wallet has a seed phrase i.e a string of 12-24 words associated with it. Losing access to these seedpharses could lead to lose of all the assets in the wallet, forever.


Recovery method kicks in, in case of the wallet going wrong or forgetting the password. But due to the highly in frequent usage of seedphrase in recovery method, users mismanage their seedphrases leading to irreversible loss of funds.

​

Solution:

A hardware wallet that needs no seedphrase backup for the user to stress over.

​

Product Features:

  • No Seedphrase backup required
    Cypherock shards or distributes the seedphrases into 5 company provided wallet components. User requires only 2 of the 5 shards to recover their funds.
  • First decentralized hardware wallet
    Since no single component contains the whole seedphrase, hence, there is No single point of faliure making it impossible for a hacker to gain access to the seedphrase of the wallet.
  • Open source code with dedicated secure element
    The source code of the wallet security is publicly accessible via github. Hence, users with technical know how's can check safety for themselves.
  • 4 wallets in 1
    Each Cypherock X1 has 4 seedphrase slots in comparision to other hardware wallets that just have one slot. Hence, each X1 is equivalent to 4 hardware wallets.
  • Backup your other hardware wallets on Cypherock to make them seedless
    You can backup 4 seedphrases of any other wallet onto the Cypherock X1, thereby, making them seed-less as well.

    If you back up your all other wallet's seedphrases onto your X1 then it unlocks the portfolio management capability i.e you can manage all those backed up wallets from inside your cysync
    Problem Solved: Less hassle of managing multiple wallets from multiple platforms.

Feedback driven channels: Referral Program & Product Integrations

​

Reasoning on which acquisition channel to choose:

​Product Integrations

Why choose Product Integrations:

  • Cost-Effective: Product integrations are usually free & hence a cheaper way to acquire people.
  • Discoverability: Once integrated the DApps show product logo to other non-users of our product. Hence, increasing discoverability along with building trust & credibility in the product.
  • Customer insights: If a customer is using Dapps via Cypherock. It means user has used majority of the features of the product & can be considered an activated customer. Hence, their feedback is of utmost importance.

Why not product integration:

  • Does not solve cold start problem: Even though it's a great way to build trust & credibility but it does not provide the nudge to go buy the product.
  • Product integrations need multiple features of the product to be working seamlessly and for a new product there could be multiple failiure points in the flow.

​

Referral Program

​Why choose Referral Program:

  • Cost-Effective: Referral programs is budget friendly.
  • Trust and Credibility through Word of Mouth: Customers are more likely to trust a recommendation from someone they know.
  • Network Effects: Referrals create a network effect, where each new customer has the potential to bring in more customers, leading to exponential growth. Especially important for Pre-PMF companies.
  • Builds Loyalty: Offering incentives for referrals can increase customer loyalty. Those who refer others are likely to feel more invested in the product, and the referred customers, having received a benefit, may also show higher engagement and retention rates.

​

​

Out of these, Product Integrations is the best choice for feedback on wallet integration with the DAPPs but fails at solving the cold start problem.

Hence, the best option that is CHEAPER, FASTER & FEEDBACK DRIVEN is Referral Program.
