​What does SustVest do?

➡️ SustVest is an investment platform that allows retail investors to invest in fractional ownership of renewable projects like solar or EV with a ticket size as low as starting from just INR 5000. SustVest’s mission is to make green energy investing affordable and accessible. With better returns than FDs, Mutual Funds and being immune to market trends unlike the stock market, SustVest aims to introduce an alternative investment class to investors that will positively impact the environment while earning passive returns.

**Post Event Engagement Strategy**

Survey questions for speakers ⤵️
  1. Did the provided technology for your presentation (e.g., AV equipment, microphone) meet your expectations in terms of adequacy and reliability? (Yes/No)
  2. Are you open to the possibility of participating as a speaker in a forthcoming event organized by the same group? (Yes/No)
  3. Did you find the schedule of your presentation and the overall event to be efficiently organized? (Yes/No)
  4. On a scale of 1 to 5, how positively was your presentation received by the audience? (1 - Poorly received, 5 - Very well received)



Survey questions for attendees ⤵️

  1. Please rate the overall quality of the event on a scale from 1 to 5.
  2. What was your favorite aspect of the masterclass?
  3. Were there any aspects of this event that you didn't find satisfactory?
  4. Would you consider attending a future edition of this event?
  5. Did you acquire the knowledge you intended to gain from attending?
  6. How probable is it that you will reach out to individuals you connected with during the event for networking purposes?
