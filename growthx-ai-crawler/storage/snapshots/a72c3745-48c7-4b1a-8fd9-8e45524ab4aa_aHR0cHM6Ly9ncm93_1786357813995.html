About Smartlead

Smartlead.ai is a powerful cold email automation platform designed to help businesses scale their outreach efforts and drive significant revenue growth. Our mission is to turn email outreach into a reliable, data-driven revenue engine by enabling users to engage with prospects more effectively and at scale.

Founded in 2022 by Vaibhav Namburi, Smartlead has quickly grown into top 4% of the fastest-growing AI-powered SaaS companies in the world, serving over 10,000 users across various industries. Our platform simplifies the complex process of email outreach by offering seamless integrations, advanced inbox placement tests, and mailbox monitoring to ensure top-notch deliverability and results.

Smartlead is trusted by lead generation agencies, sales teams, recruiters, and marketers worldwide to boost lead engagement, improve email deliverability, and maximize conversions. We’re a bootstrapped, profitable startup, consistently pushing the boundaries of what outreach automation can achieve.

At Smartlead, we are proud of our innovative culture, global remote team, and dedication to helping our customers succeed.

Users

Smartlead primarily serves businesses focused on cold email outreach, lead generation, and sales automation. Our customers range from lead gen agencies to sales teams and recruiters looking to improve their outreach efforts and drive revenue growth.

Market

We are positioned in the SaaS market, particularly focusing on outreach automation and email marketing. The lead generation space is competitive, and our main competitors are companies offering cold email solutions and CRM systems with outreach features.

Smartlead.ai is an advanced email automation platform that allows businesses to scale their cold email outreach while ensuring deliverability and personalization. Our product’s core strength is its ability to integrate with other tools and provide mailbox monitoring, inbox placement tests, and warmup features to ensure better outreach success rates.

Introduction to the Role: Social Media Manager

As the Social Media Manager at Smartlead, you play a pivotal role in amplifying our brand’s voice, increasing online visibility, and building meaningful connections with our audience.

You will be responsible for developing and executing a comprehensive social media strategy that aligns with Smartlead’s business objectives. Your work will directly influence how our brand is perceived, foster community engagement, and drive lead generation efforts.

Who​

As the Social Media Manager at Smartlead, you’ll drive our online presence, engage with our community, and collaborate with teams across marketing, product, design, and customer success. You’ll also work with influencers and partners to help expand our reach and build meaningful connections.

​

What

Your primary goal is to increase website traffic from social media by 3X and boost signups by 2X over the next six months (by the end of Q3).


Why

Social media plays a vital role in positioning Smartlead as a leader in cold email automation. Your work will keep our audience engaged and excited while driving key business outcomes like lead generation and customer acquisition.

We've seen a clear link between social media engagement and increased website traffic, which directly leads to more signups.

​


Objective

Our social media strategy centers on building a strong online presence, engaging with our audience and driving brand awareness.

We want our SMM to focus on creating valuable content loops that resonates with our target audience and encourages interaction; therfore drving us towards the goal.

  1. Increase Brand Awareness:
    • We aim to expand our reach and ensure more people know about our brand. This involves growing our follower base across all platforms and increasing our online visibility.
    • We aim to increase our LinkedIn follower base from 25K to 35K by the end of Q3 through organic efforts.
  3. Engage Our Audience:
    • Engagement is key, and we want to foster a community where our followers feel connected and valued - respond to comments promptly, participate in conversations and tags where Smartlead is mentioned, and focus on creating user-generated content.
  5. Drive Traffic and Signups to Our Website:
    • One of our main objectives is to use social media to drive traffic to our website.
    • As mentioned above, we aim to increase website traffic from social media by 3X and boost signups by 2X over the next six months (by the end of Q3).
  7. Monitor and Improve:
    • Continuous improvement is crucial. Regularly analyze social media performance, monitor trends, and adjust our strategy based on data and feedback.
    • Eg. The current trend is Linkedin is pushing out more short-form video content on the channel; similar to IG and YouTube. A little effort now can drive us huge impressions and engagement because of changing algorithms.

​


Role and Responsibilities

As the Social Media Manager, your primary mission is to develop and execute content strategies that amplify brand awareness, drive engagement, and generate leads. We're seeking someone self-driven, adaptable, and ready to thrive in a fast-paced, remote environment.
  1. Strategy Development and Execution:
    • Develop and implement the overall social media strategy.
    • Align social media activities with business goals and marketing objectives.
  3. Content Creation and Management:
    • Create, curate, and manage content across all social media platforms.
    • Develop a monthly social media content calendar.
  5. Brand Voice and Consistency:
    • Ensure consistency in the brand voice and visual elements across all social media channels.
    • Develop and maintain a Brand Guideline for Smartlead.
  7. Community Engagement and Management:
    • Engage with the online community, responding to comments and messages.
    • Develop and implement community management strategies and campaigns.
    • Foster relationships and nurture community members.
  9. Performance Analysis and Reporting:
    • Analyze social media metrics to measure the success of campaigns.
    • Create and present monthly performance reports.
    • Use insights to optimize content and strategies.
  11. Collaboration:
    • Work closely with the design team to enhance the visual appeal of social media content.
    • Collaborate with the marketing team to align social media efforts with broader campaigns.
    • Establish connections and collaborate with key team members in marketing, design, and other relevant departments.
  13. Influencer Partnerships:
    • Identify and establish partnerships with influencers to expand reach and engagement.
  15. Crisis Management:
    • Handle negative comments and crises on social media professionally.
    • Provide excellent customer service through social media channels.

​

Key Performance Indicators (KPIs)

To measure our social media success, we focus on the following KPIs:
  1. Follower Growth: The number of new followers we gain over a specific period (e.g., 35K followers by the end of Q3)
  2. Engagement Rate: Tags by users/customers, likes, comments, shares, and overall interaction with our posts. (e.g: 30-35% increase in impressions, 25% increase in post reactions, 20-25% increase in the number of tags and mentions)
  3. Website Traffic: The traffic driven from social media to our website (e.g., increase social traffic to website by 3X).
  4. Signups: The number of signups through social media campaigns (e.g., a 2X increase in signups to free trials).
  5. Revenue: Monthly recurring revenue from social media efforts (hitting our MRR and LTV targets per month).
  6. Brand Sentiment: Numbers of tags and posts mentioning Smartlead use case for their growth.

30-60-90 Day Plan

This 30-60-90 day plan is designed to give you the context, clarity, and confidence to thrive in your role as Social Media Manager at Smartlead.
The plan outlines your key responsibilities, milestones, and outcomes for the first three months. By following this, you’ll gain a strong understanding of the brand, the tools you’ll use, the team you’ll collaborate with, and the social media strategy you’ll be leading.

The goal is to help you effectively manage and elevate Smartlead’s online presence while contributing to business growth.


What to Expect​

• A deep dive into Smartlead’s social media landscape.

• Opportunities to develop a content strategy that drives engagement and traffic.

• Clear deliverables and milestones for each phase to track your progress.

• Collaboration with key team members to fully understand our brand, product, and audience.


What This Will Help You With​

• Building a solid foundation of the brand, product, and target market.

• Developing the right strategies for user engagement and lead generation.

• Building relationships with internal teams for efficient collaboration.

• Delivering immediate value through data-driven social media strategies.


Next Steps​

• Follow the milestones and expectations outlined in each phase.

• Set up introductory meetings with team members.
