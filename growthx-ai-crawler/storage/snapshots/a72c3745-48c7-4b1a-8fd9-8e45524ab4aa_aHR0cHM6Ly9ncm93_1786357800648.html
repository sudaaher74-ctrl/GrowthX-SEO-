📖 What is HealthifyMe?

HealthifyMe offers a comprehensive solution to achieve health and fitness goals with various features like food, workout, water, sleep trackers, and home workout videos curated for Indian audience. It introduces innovative tools like HealthifySnap for image-based food recognition and real-time calorie tracking. Users can participate in live video classes, receive personalized diet plans, and track progress through detailed health data and AI-powered insights through Ria. The app also integrates with Apple Health and wearable devices for seamless activity tracking.

Core Features:

  • Comprehensive Tracking: Track food intake, workouts, water, sleep, and even handwashing
  • HealthifySnap: Image-based food recognition for easy calorie tracking tailored for Indian audience
  • Personalized Guidance: Receive insights and tailored advice from AI coach 'Ria' and real-time feedback during live classes..
  • Pro Coaching: Offers professional coaching through access to nutritionists, fitness trainers, and mental wellness experts.
  • Wearable Integration: Syncs with fitness devices for seamless tracking.
  • HealthifySense: Focuses on mental wellness, providing a holistic health solution.
  • ​Community Engagement: Participate in challenges and live video classes with a global fitness community.

Uniqueness: HealthifyMe’s deep focus on Indian dietary needs and its comprehensive health management approach distinguish it from global competitors like MyFitnessPal.

Defining Core EnR Metrics

​

Engagement Framework

Key Tracking Metric

Selected

Rationale

Frequency

Number of times users log meals, workouts, or sleep.

Primary

Regular tracking reinforces the habit and leads to more accurate insights, helping users see progress and stay engaged.

Depth

Amount of time spent engaging with personalized plans and live sessions.

NA

Spending more time doesn't necessarily increase the perceived value without consistent outcomes.

Breadth

Number of features (e.g., AI coaching, live classes, multiple trackers) used by a user.

Secondary

Using multiple features can enhance the user's experience by providing a more comprehensive view of their health.

Insights

  • Frequency: Essential for maintaining consistency in tracking health and fitness, leading to tangible results and higher user satisfaction.
  • Breadth: Important for enhancing the user experience by exploring multiple features like AI coaching and live classes, helping achieve health goals more effectively.
  • Depth: Secondary focus; while beneficial, regular engagement and feature usage are more crucial for driving value.
  • Prioritization: Focus on frequency to build habits, followed by breadth for a comprehensive experience, with depth supporting committed users.

Engagement Campaigns

​

Campaign : "HealthifyMe Adventure Tracker"​

  • Goal: Convert casual users to core users by making fitness tracking engaging through gamified elements.
  • User Persona: Casual and Core Users
  • Pitch/Content: "Embark on a fitness adventure—track your daily activities to earn rewards and unlock new levels!"
  • Theme: Gamified fitness tracking
  • Offer: In-app rewards, special badges
  • Channels: In-app notifications, push notifications
  • Frequency & Timing: Ongoing, with monthly adventure resets
  • Success Metrics:
    • 20% increase in daily activity tracking
    • Increase in active user retention
  • Milestones:
    • Monthly adventure resets with new challenges
    • Periodic review of user engagement metrics
    • Introduction of new levels and rewards based on user feedback
  • Hypothesis: Gamifying fitness tracking will boost engagement by making it fun and rewarding to track daily activities.
  • Details: The campaign will focus on daily activity tracking, where users can earn rewards and badges as they progress through different levels. New challenges and adventures will be introduced every month to keep users engaged.
  • Target Feature: Adventure Tracker
  • Proposed Flow: Users set up daily fitness goals → Track activities through the app → Earn points and unlock new levels → Redeem rewards for achieving milestones.

​

Campaign : "Sunday Mystery Meal"

  • Goal: Encourage regular engagement by introducing users to new and diverse healthy recipes.
  • User Persona: Food Enthusiasts
  • Pitch/Content: "Discover a new, healthy recipe mystery every Sunday to expand your culinary skills!"
  • Theme: Weekly surprise healthy recipe
  • Offer: Cooking gadgets, in-app currency for participation
  • Channels: Email, social media, in-app notifications
  • Frequency & Timing: Weekly, with new recipes revealed every Sunday
  • Success Metrics:
    • 10% increase in recipe section usage
    • Increase in user-generated recipes
  • Milestones:
    • Weekly reveal of new recipes
    • Increased participation in recipe sharing
    • Community engagement through cooking challenges
  • Hypothesis: Encouraging users to explore new healthy recipes will increase app engagement and diversify their meal choices.
  • Details: The campaign will unveil a new healthy recipe every Sunday, enticing users to try out and share their culinary creations. The initiative aims to build a community around healthy eating by encouraging user participation.
  • Target Feature: Sunday Mystery Meal
  • Proposed Flow: Users receive a new recipe each Sunday → Try and share their version of the recipe → Earn in-app currency and unlock HealthifyMe products as rewards(SmartScale, protein powder , etc) → Participate in community challenges.

Campaign : "Buddy Fitness Challenge"​

Goal: Increase engagement by encouraging users to work out with friends, creating a sense of community and accountability.

User Persona: Core and Casual Users

Pitch/Content: "Team up with your fitness buddy and conquer challenges together for double the rewards!"

Theme: Social workout challenges with friends

Offer: Bonus rewards for both users, special badges, leaderboard recognition

Channels: In-app notifications, push notifications, email

Frequency & Timing: Ongoing, with weekly challenges

Success Metrics:

  • 30% increase in buddy-based workout sessions
  • Improved user retention through social engagement

Milestones:

  • Weekly introduction of new buddy challenges
  • Monthly review of participation and success rates

Hypothesis: Working out with a friend increases motivation and app usage by creating a shared fitness journey.

Details: Users pair up with friends to take on fitness challenges together, earning double rewards when both complete the task.

Target Feature: Buddy Workout Tracker

Proposed Flow: Users invite a buddy → Accept the challenge together → Track workouts → Earn rewards for completing the challenge together.

Campaign : "Healthy Habit Streaks"

​Goal: Build user engagement through consistent habit tracking.

User Persona: Core and Power Users

Pitch/Content: "Keep your healthy habits streak alive and earn exclusive rewards!"

Theme: Habit Formation & Rewards

Offer: Discounts, personalized coaching, streak badges

Channels: Push Notifications, In-App Messages, Emails

Frequency & Timing: Daily tracking with weekly/monthly rewards

Success Metrics:

  • 30% increase in habit tracking
  • Improved retention

Milestones:

  • 7-Day Streak: Extra points or badges
  • 30-Day Streak: Discounts or free features
  • 90-Day Streak: Personalized coaching

Hypothesis: Building a habit through daily streaks will increase user retention by fostering consistent engagement.

Details: The campaign rewards users for maintaining daily health habits, creating a sense of achievement and motivation. The longer the streak, the better the rewards, encouraging sustained app usage.

Target Feature: Daily Habit Tracker

Proposed Flow: Users set daily health habits → Track habits daily → Earn rewards for maintaining streaks → Unlock bigger rewards as streaks continue.
