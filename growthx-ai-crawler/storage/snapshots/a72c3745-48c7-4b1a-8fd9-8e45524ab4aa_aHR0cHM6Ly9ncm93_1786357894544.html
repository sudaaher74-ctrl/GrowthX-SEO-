1. Understanding the Audience

Since the target audience (general contractors, estimators, etc.) isn’t necessarily active on social media, it’s important to meet them where they are. Some alternative channels to consider:

  • Industry-specific webinars: Many GCs and subcontractors attend webinars to learn about tools and best practices.
  • Trade publications: Construction industry magazines or online publishers like Engineering News-Record or Construction Executive are great platforms to reach this audience.
  • Expos and Trade Shows: Construction expos (like World of Concrete or CONEXPO-CON/AGG) are prime locations to engage face-to-face.
  • Email newsletters: Many construction professionals prefer receiving information via email or newsletters.
2. Finding the Right Type of Loop for Beam AI

To create an effective content loop, Beam AI focuses on three key components:

Hook

We lead with solution-driven messages that address real pain points:

  • “Save hours on every bid with automated takeoffs.”
  • “Streamline your estimating process with AI-powered accuracy.”

Content Creators

  • Users: We showcase success stories and testimonials from contractors who have improved their bidding process using Beam AI.
  • Influencers: Beam AI partners with industry experts, consultants, and construction project managers who can advocate for the product.
  • Brand: Through case studies, demo videos, and webinars, Beam AI demonstrates real-world impact.

Distribution Channels

  • Webinars: We host live demos and educational sessions, promoted through construction-focused email lists and communities.
  • Trade Publications: Beam AI invests in sponsored articles, case studies, and ads in highly trusted construction media.
  • Expos and Trade Shows: Live product demonstrations at major expos help Beam AI generate high-quality leads.
  • Email Campaigns: Segmented campaigns offering actionable content like "10 Ways to Improve Your Bid Accuracy" keep audiences engaged.



3. Creation a Flow Diagram

Here’s an updated content flow diagram for Beam AI:

  • 1. Engagement →
  • A contractor attends a Beam AI webinar, reads a case study, or visits our booth at an expo.
  • 2. Content Creation →
  • Beam AI demonstrates automation capabilities through real-world case studies, webinars, and live demos.
  • 3. Sharing →
  • Satisfied users and attendees share Beam AI’s content with peers, expanding reach organically.
  • 4. Distribution →

Beam AI amplifies the content across emails, industry sites, and future expos — continuously fueling the engagement cycle.


4. Gathering Insights and Improving

As Beam AI launches these content loops, we actively track performance to optimize:

  • Webinar Feedback: We gather attendee insights to understand key pain points and product appeal.
  • Publisher Metrics: We analyze traffic, engagement, and lead generation from ads and sponsored articles.

This ongoing feedback loop helps Beam AI double down on the most effective strategies and channels.

🏗️ Referral & Partner Program Strategy (B2B SaaS – Construction Tech)

🎯 Goal:

Leverage external networks (consultants, suppliers, and industry influencers) to drive high-quality leads and revenue through structured referral and reseller models.

1. 🎢 Program Models Overview

➤ Referral Program

  • Who it’s for: Consultants, advisors, and influencers in the construction space.
  • How it works: Introduce clients → Our sales team handles the close.
  • Incentive: 5–10% of first-year revenue (tiered to reward repeat referrals).
  • Risk Level: Low (no billing, no support required).
  • Ideal for: Lightweight advocates or advisors with strong networks.

➤ Reseller Program

  • Who it’s for: Value-added resellers or suppliers.
  • How it works: They resell our product, bill the client, and may provide support.
  • Incentive: 20–30% discount off MSRP.
  • Risk Level: Moderate (more involvement in sales and support).
  • Ideal for: Strategic partners who want deeper integration.

2. 📦 Onboarding & Enablement

✅ Welcome Kit

  • Digital kit: welcome letter, key product value props, referral process, contact info.
  • Free trial/demo account to build familiarity and authenticity.

✅ Product Training

  • Live training session (1:1 or group webinar).
  • Demo walkthrough + practical use cases (e.g., drywall contractor estimating).
  • Optional certification quiz → “Certified Partner” status.

✅ Sales & Marketing Collateral

  • Partner playbook (ideal customer profile, objection handling).
  • Customer-facing assets: demo video links, brochures, slides, case studies.
  • Shared resource folder (Google Drive or basic partner portal).

3. 💻 Platform & Process

🔑 Partner Portal

  • Basic version: website landing page + form for deal submissions.
  • Future-ready: scalable PRM (PartnerStack, Impartner).

🧭 Internal Alignment

  • Assign a Partner Manager.
  • Align sales with incentive structures (quota credit, SPIFFs).
  • Process for tracking and resolving deal registration conflicts.
