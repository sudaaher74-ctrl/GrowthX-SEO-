Understanding Mudrex

Mudrex is a crypto investment and trading platform in India that aims to simplify accessibility to cryptocurrencies. It provides users with the tools to invest in curated crypto portfolios, trade individual tokens, and trade in crypto derivatives. With compliance, security, and simplicity at its core, Mudrex makes crypto investing accessible to both first-time and experienced users.


Core Value Proposition

The core value proposition of Mudrex is to simplify and empower crypto investing for Indians by providing structured investment and trading products. It enables users to confidently grow their assets with minimal effort and maximum transparency. Whether through passive investing in curated coins and coin sets or active trading in crypto futures and spot market, Mudrex gives users access to diverse, secure, and easy-to-use tools for building crypto portfolios.


Key Features:

  • Curated crypto investment products like Coin Sets (thematic baskets)
  • Access to crypto tokens and futures trading for experienced traders
  • INR and crypto deposits/withdrawals with seamless UPI
  • Performance tracking and portfolio analytics
  • Regulatory-compliant and secure funds


Key Problems Solved for User:

  • Crypto investing is confusing and risky for new and casual users
  • Onboarding with INR is slow, clunky, or not seamless
  • Trading platforms are complex and intimidating to navigate
  • Lack of trust and confidence in the security of crypto platforms


User Experience

Users experience Mudrex’s core value proposition repeatedly when they:

  • Invest in Coins and Coin Sets
  • Actively trade spot and futures to capitalise on market movements
  • Deposit INR or crypto funds into their wallet to start investing/trading
  • Withdraw profits or funds smoothly through supported rails
  • Get insights about crypto investments and market trends


This experience is designed to reinforce trust, ease of use, and consistent value delivery whether users are long-term investors or short-term traders.

Defining Active Users

An active user on Mudrex is someone who performs any transaction, trade or investment on the platform within their natural frequency.

On a granular level, these include:

  • INR deposit or withdrawal
  • Crypto deposit or withdrawal
  • Investment in Coins/ Coin Sets
  • Spot or Futures trade (open/close)


These actions reflect genuine engagement with the financial core of the platform rather than passive browsing.

Who are these Customers?

To arrive at a better understanding of the users, we conducted user calls again to understand user's perspectives, experiences and pain points. The aim is to understand:

  • What value retained users are deriving consistently
  • What aspects of the product experience are working well for them
  • What prevents them from increasing engagement further (if any)
  • How their needs or usage patterns have evolved
  • What features or communication really resonate with them
  • What emotional or functional triggers drive their continued usage


Based on these calls, insights were derived to support the segmentation and hence the campaigns. These insights are derived from conversations and patterns observed among retained users — those who have crossed onboarding and are now regularly using the platform. They reflect motivations, habits, evolving needs, and emerging pain points. These help refine ICPs, segment the users and drive retention strategy to deepen product engagement.

User Background & Profile

  • Most retained users started with small investments and have gradually increased exposure over time.
  • Users span both casual investors (long-term passive plays) and active traders (frequent buyers, futures traders).
  • A growing portion of users are multi-platform crypto users—they compare Mudrex with others on UX, returns, and convenience.
  • Some retained users originally came via referrals or content and are now advocates, inviting peers to join.
  • Mobile-first behaviour is common — most users check their investments casually on the go.

User Goals & Motivations

  • Consistency in Returns – Retained users want performance that beats traditional finance or matches expectations they’ve built over time.
  • Hands-off Investing – Many use recurring investments and coin sets to simplify their wealth creation journey.
  • Confidence & Visibility – As their investment size grows, they expect more transparency, quick settlements, and fund security.

Pain Points and Issues

  • Lack of Clarity & Confidence - Users often feel unsure about which product to use or how to invest, leading to hesitation and inactivity.
  • Low Perceived Value or Returns - When users don’t see tangible gains or value from their investments, they lose motivation to continue.
  • High Friction from Compliance & Complexity - Onboarding processes like KYC, along with a complex product experience, discourage users from staying active.
  • Weak Habit Formation & Engagement Hooks- Users struggle to make investing a routine due to lack of personalised nudges, timely insights, or engaging features.
  • Fear of Volatility & Regulatory Uncertainty - The dynamic nature of crypto markets and concerns around legality or safety create a psychological barrier to deeper investment.

Other Key Considerations

  • Value is Realised Through Habit – Weekly or monthly investment behaviour keeps users sticky. Breaks in this pattern risk churn.
  • Trust Builds with Predictability – Smooth fund flow, regular returns, and clear communication are critical for emotional trust.
  • Users Seek Learning & Growth – As retained users mature, they look for education, advanced features, or higher-yield options.

Product Hook

Goal

To drive engagement and increase investment activity among users by turning crypto investing from a one-time action into a repeat behaviour.


Success Metric

  • Increase in frequency of investments per user
  • Improved amount of investment over time
  • Higher conversion rate from casual → core → power users


Problem Statement

Most users drop off or become passive after their first few transactions because:

  • They don’t understand what to invest in
  • They don’t see immediate gains
  • The process doesn’t feel rewarding


Current Alternative

  • Manual investment across exchanges or apps with little guidance
  • Following influencers/YouTube for tips


Solution (Hook)

Create a “Dynamic Portfolio Nudging Loop” — a personalized, insight-driven investment assistant that:

  • Learns user preferences
  • Sends actionable, curated investment ideas (like trending coinsets or top trader signals)
  • Rewards consistent activity through progress streaks, insights, and micro-wins


How this will work:

Trigger → Curated Insight/Opportunity → Easy Investment Action → Reinforcement (Insight/Reward)

Repeat this loop with evolving personalisation and habit-building nudges.


  1. Trigger: User gets a timely, personalized notification — “Top 3 coinsets with strong 7-day growth”
  2. Insight: Click opens the app with bite-sized insights (why it’s trending, risk level, returns)
  3. Action: One-tap invest (user selects amount, confirms)
  4. Reinforcement: Post-investment feedback — “You’re ahead of 80% of investors this week!”
  5. Loop: User receives weekly portfolio boosts, insights, or rewards, forming a habit


Metrics to Track

  • % of users engaging with investment nudges
  • Avg number of investments per user per week
  • Retention by segment (casual/core/power)
  • % movement from one segment to next
  • CTR and conversion from nudge to action

Micro Insights

  • Organic (SEO) - high intent users

Worst Channels:
