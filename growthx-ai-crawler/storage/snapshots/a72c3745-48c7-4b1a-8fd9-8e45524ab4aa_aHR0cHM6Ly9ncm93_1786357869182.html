📊 CAC:LTV Calculation (in USD):

Assumptions:

  • CAC (Customer Acquisition Cost) = $50 (ad spend, content creation, and tools per acquired user)
  • ARPU (Average Revenue Per User) = $25/month
  • Average User Lifespan = 24 months


Calculation:

  • LTV (Lifetime Value) = ARPU × User Lifespan = $25 × 24 = $600
  • CAC:LTV Ratio = CAC / LTV = $50 / $600 = 1:12


Interpretation:

  • A ratio of 1:12 indicates a high return on ad spend, validating the use of programmatic ads.

🧠 Key Insights:

  • Mudrex Advantage: INR deposits/withdrawals & wallet-to-wallet transfers.
  • Creative Focus: Use real-time crypto trends for engaging ads.
  • Performance Metrics: CTR, CAC:LTV ratio, and conversion rates.





🤝 Partner (Affiliate) Program: The UGC-Powered Growth Engine

Mudrex doesn't follow the traditional partner or referral programs. Instead, we’ve redefined affiliate marketing by turning it into a User-Generated Content (UGC)-driven growth engine. Our partners aren't just promoters; they're active participants in the crypto community, sharing their real experiences and insights.

🧩 Partner Segments

  1. 👨‍🏫 Crypto Gurus
    Crypto influencers like Pushpendra and Kashif Raza lead this segment. They:
    • Promote Mudrex through their social platforms like X (Twitter) and YouTube.
    • Participate in Mudrex-sponsored events, providing educational content and market insights.
    • Leverage their referral links and promo codes to onboard their engaged followers.
  3. 💼 Fund Managers
    Fund managers with crypto-savvy clients play a key role by:
    • Recommending Mudrex as a trusted investment platform.
    • Using personalized referral links or promo codes for their client network.
    • Sharing portfolio performance and insights through presentations and webinars.
  5. 🎥 YouTube Streamers & Racers
    YouTube streamers who conduct live trading sessions use Mudrex as their go-to platform. They:
    • Stream live trades and showcase their profit margins to build credibility.
    • Share their referral links directly with subscribers via video descriptions and pinned comments.
    • Offer exclusive promo codes during live Q&A sessions to drive engagement.

🚀 Why This Program Works

  • Authenticity: Real users share real experiences, making the content more relatable.
  • UGC-Driven: Every trade, profit screenshot, or shared experience acts as organic marketing.
  • Multi-Platform Reach: Content is spread across YouTube, X, Telegram, and event collaborations.
