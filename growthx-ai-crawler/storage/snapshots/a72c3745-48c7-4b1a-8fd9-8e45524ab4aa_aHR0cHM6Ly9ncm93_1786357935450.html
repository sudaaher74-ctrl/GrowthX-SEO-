Section - 1 : Company overview, features, problem it solves

What is BookMyShow?

BookMyShow is an entertainment discovery and ticketing platform that helps users:

  • Discover movies
  • Book movie tickets
  • Book events (concerts, comedy shows, sports)
  • Purchase activity experiences
  • View trailers and ratings
  • Receive recommendations
  • Manage bookings

Movie & Cinema - 75–78% market share in online movie ticketing. 94.3 crore tickets sold in 2023 across ~10,000 cinemas. Box office hit ₹11,997 cr in 2024.
Live Events - ~55% share in live event ticketing. 30,687 events across 319 cities in 2024. Owns Lollapalooza India. 682% growth in Tier 2 cities.
BMS Stream & More - TVoD (transactional video-on-demand). Gift cards, activities, workshops, plays. International presence: Malaysia, Singapore, Indonesia.

Section - 2 : Core Value Prop, Natural Frequency, Engagement Framework

Core Value Prop

"The experience starts here"

For entertainment-hungry Indians, BookMyShow is the single, trusted gateway for users to discover and book entertainment experiences effortlessly, ensuring they never miss the movies, events, and experiences they care about.


Multi-Entertainment Platform - Movies, concerts, sports, stand-up comedy, theatre, festivals, and activities—all in one app.
Convenience + seat control - Book the exact seat, at 2am, in 90 seconds. M-ticket means no printout ever.
Financial rewards - Bank BOGOs, BMS Insider points, co-branded card offers. ~30% of transactions via bank promotions.
Trust - Authentic tickets, secure payments, and reliable support
Cultural passport - From ₹100 single-screen to ₹50,000 VIP concert - BMS spans every entertainment tier.
Personalized Discovery - Get recommendations, trending events, location-based suggestions, and release reminders.
Food & Beverage Pre-Booking - Skip queues by ordering snacks before reaching the venue
Early Access & Exclusives - Priority booking for concerts, blockbuster releases, and premium events.
Hyperlocal Event Discovery - Find nearby events and experiences based on city, interests, and past bookings.

What is the natural frequency of BookMyShow?

Based on primary and secondary research done with users we can categorise users as per the below frequency:

Power Users (Movies) : Average cinephile watches ~6 films/year = ~0.5× monthly; heavy users are 6–8×

Core (Live events) : High-consideration; planned well in advance. Discovery + reminder loops are critical.

Casual (BMS Stream / TVoD) : Triggered by new releases. Bridges the gap between transactional visits.

🟢 Breadth of Engagement: (WINNER) - BookMyShow's value comes from enabling as many users as possible to discover and book entertainment. Growth is primarily driven by increasing the number of active users and bookers rather than making existing users spend more time in the app.

🟡 Frequency of Engagement: Users naturally book movies and events only when they have a need, making usage event-driven rather than habitual. While repeat bookings are important for retention, they occur less frequently than engagement in daily-use products.

🔴 Depth of Engagement: A successful BookMyShow experience is often quick—users find a show and book within minutes. Therefore, higher time spent or more screens viewed does not necessarily indicate higher value or engagement.

Section 4: Product Hooks and Campaigns to drive Engagement

Product Hook

BMS Entertainment Pulse


A personalised, always-on entertainment feed that gives users a reason to open BookMyShow even when they have nothing to book.

Goal

Increase monthly active open rate from a booking-only trigger to a weekly habit. Move median session frequency from 1×/month to 3×/month without requiring a purchase.

Success Metrics

Primary:

  • Monthly Active Users (MAU)
  • Repeat Visits/User

Secondary:

  • Watchlist Creation Rate
  • Notification CTR
  • Repeat Booking Rate

North Star:

  • % Users Returning Within 30 Days

Problem Statement

Between two transactions, BookMyShow has no reason to exist for 78% of its users. The app is opened only when a user has already decided to go out - meaning BMS never influences the decision to go out. It loses the most valuable moment: when someone is bored on a Tuesday and wondering what to do this weekend.

Current Alternative

Users currently get entertainment discovery from:

Instagram / Reels

Trailers, artist posts, friend check-ins

WhatsApp groups

"Anyone up for something this weekend?"

Word of mouth

Colleague / friend recommendations

Google Search

"movies releasing this week"

Solution

Introduce BMS Pulse — a personalised entertainment feed on the app home screen, powered by the user's interest graph (booking history + wishlists + tracked artists + location + friends' activity). It surfaces: trailers of upcoming films matching taste, "X friends are going to this" social proof cards, artist tour alerts, "Only 12 seats left" urgency nudges, and a weekly curated "Your weekend, sorted" digest. The feed is non-transactional by design — it builds the habit of opening BMS, and lets booking emerge naturally from discovery.

Full rollout - all users. Launch social layer (friends' activity). Begin Pulse → campaign tie-ins with campaigns below.

Section 5 : Retention And Churn

Retention Curve

The numbers below are calibrated from industry benchmarks: BookMyShow's publicly reported DAU/MAU patterns, Paytm/Zomato cohort disclosures, and OTT/ticketing norms from Amplitude and Mixpanel industry reports. The steepest drop happens in the first 7 days, this is the "habit not yet formed" cliff.

  • Day 1 Retention - 38% (Industry Avg. - 25 - 40%)
  • Day 7 Retention - 22% (Industry Avg. - 15-25%)
  • Day 30 Retention - 14% (Industry Avg. - 8-15%)
  • Curve Flatlines at Day 60 - 8-9% Steady State

Three zones tell the story. The cliff (D0–D7) is where ~60–72% of new users vanish, no habit formed, no reason to return. The decay zone (D7–D30) is where casual users mostly disappear and core users thin out. The flatline after Day 60 represents the genuinely retained base - about 8–9% overall, dominated by Power users.

Churn Reasons: Voluntary vs Involuntary

No.1 churn driver: no upcoming intent - BMS is transactional by nature. Users leave not from dissatisfaction, but because there's nothing planned. The product has no pull between transactions.

Voluntary Churn

No intent / nothing to book - No upcoming movie/event that excites them. Pure transactional gap. Hits casual users hardest.

Checkout friction & hidden fees - Convenience fee revealed at last step. Cart abandonment converts to churn for first-timers.

Switched to competing app - Paytm Movies, Justdial, or direct multiplex app (PVR, INOX) offering better seat selection or prices.

Poor discovery / irrelevant content - Homepage doesn't surface content matching user's past genre or city. Feels generic.

Bad post-booking experience - Cancelled show with no proactive notification, slow refund, poor support resolution.

No value between bookings - App has nothing to offer when user isn't actively booking. No feed, no content loop, no community.

Involuntary Churn

Payment failure / UPI timeout - High-frequency issue in India. Seats held but payment fails; user drops off in frustration, often permanently.

App uninstall (storage pressure) - Mid-tier Android devices. BMS gets removed in periodic storage cleanups if it's not in daily rotation.

Life stage change - New job, city relocation, baby, reduced discretionary spend. Natural attrition in 25–35 segment.

Notification fatigue - Irrelevant push notifications (wrong city, wrong genre) → blanket permission revoke. Silent churn follows.

Seasonal Null (dry content calendar) - Post-Diwali / summer slump. No big Bollywood or Hollywood releases for 4–6 weeks forces dormancy.

The most important insight here: the #1 churn driver for BMS is structural, not a product failure. Users don't leave angry - they leave because the product has no reason to be opened between bookings. That makes retention fundamentally a content/discovery problem, not just a UX problem.

Section 6: Resurrection Campaigns

Campaign 1: "Something big is coming to your city" - Event Discovery

Churn: Poor discovery for niche events

Item

Details

Segment

Core users (live events/concerts) dormant 45–120 days. Last booking was a live event or sports. City and genre preference known from history.

Pitch

Subject: [Artist/Team] is coming to [City] — and you've seen them before
Body: "Last time [Artist] played [City], you were there. They're back on [Date]. This one's bigger." Personalised using past booking history. Visual-led with event poster.

Offer

Early-bird group booking incentive: "Book 4+ tickets and get ₹200 off total." Targets the social planner persona — makes them the hero who organised the outing.

Frequency & Timing

Announce send: 4 weeks before event. Reminder: 2 weeks out ("filling fast"). Final: 3 days out with seat availability counter. Max 1 campaign per event per user.

Success Metric

CTR on event link
Group booking rate (4+ tickets)
Re-booking rate
Revenue per campaign send
D60 retention post-event

Email + Whatsapp + Push

Campaign 3: "99 & you're in" - Price led reactivation

Churn: Price sensitivity/ hidden fees at payment/ checkout friction

Item

Details

Segment

Casual users dormant 21–45 days. Last session had a cart abandonment at payment step (convenience fee drop-off). Or last booking was triggered by a deal/cashback offer.

Pitch

Subject: [Movie] for ₹99. No catch.
Body: "We know the fees add up. Not this time. Book [trending movie] for ₹99 all-in — no convenience fee, no surprises at checkout." One movie. One price. One tap. No asterisks.

Offer

₹99 flat ticket for a mid-week show (Mon–Thu) for a specified film. Zero convenience fee. Offer valid 48 hours. Targets price-sensitive re-entry without training users to always expect discounts on weekends.

Frequency & Timing

Released Tuesday morning, expires Wednesday midnight. Run for 2 films per month. Never on a blockbuster opening weekend — targets mid-leg of a film's run.

Campaign 4: "Your friends are going" - Social proof reactivation

Churn: No habit/ no social motivation surfaced by the product

Item

Details

Segment

Casual users dormant 30–75 days. App has permission to read contacts or user is in a known social cluster (same UPI group, same college city cohort, shared booking history). Age 18–28.

Pitch

Push: "3 people near you are watching [Film] this weekend."
Email: "Everyone in [City] is talking about [Film]. This weekend, [X thousand] people are going. Will you miss it?" Social proof framing — makes inaction feel like missing out, not just skipping a movie.

Offer

Group booking nudge: "Going with friends? Split the booking and save ₹30 per ticket on groups of 3+." Activates the social planner in casual users who don't self-identify as planners.

Frequency & Timing

Weekend campaign — Thursday 6pm send. One follow-up Saturday 9am if no booking. Max 2 social proof campaigns per month. Rotate city-level social proof with friend-level proof.

Success Metric

Group booking rate (3+ tickets)
City-level vs friend-level proof CTR
D14 re-booking rate

Email + Whatsapp + Push

Campaign 5: "Your superstar points are expiring" - Loyalty rescue

Churn: Habit Dropped/ no value perceived in loyalty program

Item

Details

Segment

Power users dormant 60–120 days who have ≥200 BMS Cash or SuperStar points sitting unused. Churned but not yet fully lapsed.

Pitch

Subject: ₹[X] is about to disappear from your account
Body: "You earned this. Don't let it expire unused. Your [X] BMS Cash is valid for 14 more days — here's what's showing near you this weekend."

Offer

Urgency-led: expiry reminder with countdown. Bonus: "Book this week and we'll top up another ₹75 BMS Cash." Loss aversion does the heavy lifting — no steep discount needed.

Frequency & Timing

Single trigger email at 14-day-to-expiry mark. If unopened, push notification at 7 days. Final SMS at 3 days. Never more than 3 touches per expiry cycle.
