🧪 Monetization Litmus Test For Mudrex Futures

Objective:

Understand if Mudrex Futures is ready to be monetized based on user retention, depth of engagement, and willingness to pay.

1. Retention Analysis


Retention Curve Observations:

  • Sharp initial drop from Week 0 (100%) to Week 1 (approx. 40%), indicating onboarding friction or unmet expectations.
  • Gradual decrease after Week 1, stabilizing slightly around Week 10-12 at approximately 15-20%.
  • Long-term retention (beyond Week 12) continues declining, suggesting a lack of long-term engagement hooks.

Conclusion:

  • Retention indicates failure in the litmus test. Significant churn immediately after onboarding shows users might not clearly understand product value or face early obstacles in using the product effectively.

2. Depth of Engagement Analysis

Current Situation:

  • Majority of the users are casual traders, trading infrequently (2-3 trades/month).
  • Core and power users constitute less than 50% of total active users.
  • Users predominantly engage only with basic features.

Ideal Scenario (Benchmark):

  • Over 50% of total users should be Core or Power users.
  • High-frequency engagement with multiple features, indicating deeper product utility.

Conclusion:

  • Mudrex Futures currently has shallow engagement, indicating it is not yet strongly monetizable. Users aren't fully utilizing advanced features, impacting perceived value.

3. Willingness to Pay (WTP)

Indicators of WTP:

  • Limited usage beyond free/basic functionalities.
  • High churn rate signals a mismatch between user expectations and delivered value.
  • Users have shown sensitivity towards current fees (0.1%), affecting trading frequency.

Steps to Evaluate Further:

  • Clearly identify target user segments (ICPs).
  • Conduct user interviews and surveys to understand friction points and value perception.
  • Run A/B experiments to test hypotheses around willingness to pay at different fee structures and feature access levels.

Conclusion:

  • Willingness to pay appears low at the current stage. Users have not perceived enough differentiated value to justify payment consistently.

Overall Litmus Test Result:

❌ Failed👎

Immediate Next Steps:

  • Deep dive into understanding and improving user onboarding.
  • Develop clear value communication around unique features (e.g., trade signals).
  • Focus on converting casual users to Core or Power users by educating them and enhancing perceived value.
  • Conduct pricing experiments to identify an optimal fee structure that aligns with user value perception.

Monetization Design

Based on the Monetization Litmus Test, Mudrex Futures is partially monetizing through trading fees but facing retention challenges. The retention data indicates significant drop-off, pointing towards the need for a revamped monetization strategy. The goal is clear: to monetize effectively without risking further churn.

Given my analysis:

  • High initial churn after the first few weeks indicates friction points in the onboarding and initial trading experience.
  • Long-tail retention remains low, signaling a lack of sustained engagement or compelling incentives for continued trading.

Thus, the monetization redesign will involve:

  1. Prioritizing segments with higher retention potential (Champions, Loyalists).
  2. Designing experiments that test willingness to pay and price elasticity clearly, optimizing for both short-term activation and long-term retention.

  • Why Delay Monetization for At-Risk and Hibernating Users?
    • Immediate monetization could increase churn risk. Reactivation and gradual re-engagement strategies must precede monetization.
