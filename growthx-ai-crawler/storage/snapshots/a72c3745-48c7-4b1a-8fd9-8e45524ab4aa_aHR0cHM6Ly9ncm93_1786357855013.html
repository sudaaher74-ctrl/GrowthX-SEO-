Defining product and ICPs


What is Gamma?

Gamma is an AI-powered content creation tool that enables users to quickly create presentations, documents, and webpages in a seamless and visually appealing format. It combines the power of design automation, text generation, and modern sharing capabilities to make content creation simple, fast, and engaging.


->Key features:

  • AI-driven content creation: Automatically generates presentation content based on your input text, suggesting headlines and structuring slides. 
  • Card system: Uses "cards" instead of traditional slides, allowing for easier content organization and flow. 
  • Customizable templates: Offers a wide range of pre-designed templates that can be tailored to your brand and style. 
  • Drag-and-drop interface: Easily add and rearrange elements like text, images, and videos within your presentations. 
  • Real-time collaboration: Work on presentations with multiple team members simultaneously. 
  • Multiple export formats: Download presentations in various formats like PDF, PPTX, and images. 
  • Built-in analytics: Track audience engagement with presentation views and interactions. 
  • Cross-platform accessibility: Access and edit presentations on different devices. 
  • Content refinement tools: AI-powered suggestions to improve text clarity and tone. Core problem that Gamma is solving: Gamma removes the need for manual formatting and design while creating content, allowing users to focus on their core work. The tool is tailored to individuals and teams who need to present or share information quickly without compromising on quality.


->Core Value Proposition

Create and share beautiful, interactive and professional-looking documents in minutes by using Gen AI. It does so by following- first, offering pre-designed templates that can be customized according to the content. Second, by automatically generating text and images around a topic in a well-presented layout. Third, allowing users to share the content with the colleagues.


->User experience:

Users are given option to either build documents from existing text or from existing files/webpages. Users can also generate the text and image based content from one-line prompts and share them.

  1. Ease of Use: Intuitive interface designed for both beginners and professionals, enhanced by modular card-based editing.
  2. Customizability: Offers pre-built templates and the ability to tailor content to match branding and use cases.

Pricing page design

Incorporating behavioral and cognitive biases can guide users toward a particular pricing plan, simplify the decision process, and make your product’s value more apparent.

Step 1: Pricing Page Teardown

  1. Gamma follows the three pricing strategies, with a default selection of middle tier i.e Gamma Plus. Most people will pick the second/ the middle option of the three on their screen. Thinking first is useless but the third is the most expensive.
  2. Visiting a saree shop/ownership effect: Gamma provides a freemium subscription model. Gamma's free tier experience is very good that not paying for it makes them feel like they lost something of value. Free tier experience is an experience of paid features but in a limit.
  3. Price anchoring: Decoy effect, people opting for mid-ranged Gamma Plus option, with a default set on middle option.
  4. System Design: Gamma opts for system-1 design for Gamma plus and pro which are single click checkout designed for individual customers and SMBs. RS260 ad Rs 487 per seat per user boils down to pretty low AOV. Plus, if we think about frequency, the subscriptions need monthly/annual renewals.
  5. Gamma opts for system-2 design for Enterprises. There is a lot of friction added here purposefully. If as an Enterprise, I wish to decide right plan for our team, I should go to FAQs and mail my requirements on an email.


Step 2 and Step3 : Redesigning Pricing Page and reasoning



  1. We have introduced an enterprise plan which is as of now deeply integrated into FAQs and has so much friction that its hard for users to find out. We'll still be going ahead with system-2 design for Enterprise plan.
  2. There are no nudges for users to understand which plans are ideal for which ICPs, hence we have added short description of use case and ideal user segment.
  3. We are adding scratching effect for price display to add a natural feeling of getting a better deal for users.
