What is Appreciate?

Want to invest in the U.S. in dollars? What if I told you that you could start with just ₹1? Confused? With Appreciate, you can make this a reality while keeping fees to a minimum. Excited? That's right—this platform offers you the chance to step into global investing effortlessly and affordably.

What Does Appreciate Wealth Solve For?

Appreciate provides effortless and affordable opportunities for Indians who want to diversifying their investment portfolio even beyond the borders. It solves the problem of complex and expensive investing by offering easy access to a diverse range of financial products such as US equities, mutual funds, and ETF's at minimal costs, with tools for micro-investing, goal-based planning, and automated savings.

Engagement Framework for Appreciate:

The ideal engagement framework for appreciate will be a mix of all breadth, depth and frequency.

  • Portfolio Engagement ( Frequency of Investment Activity ): The more often users interact with their portfolios, the more likely they are to increase investments, leading to higher assets under management and greater engagement with platform thereby having a revenue impact.
  • Diverse Content Consumption ( Breadth of investment activity ): 1. Exposure to various investment categories and financial product features can make users feel the platform is a comprehensive solution. The more features users explore, the more likely they are to remain engaged and less likely to seek other financial solutions and thereby increase retention.. 2. Educated users are more confident and proactive with investments, leading to deeper engagement and retention. Exposure to diverse financial knowledge areas for different financial products helps users become more informed, increases trust in the platform and give confidence to invest more and thereby diversify their portfolio which will further increase product usage and thereby retention.
  • Breadth of investment activity: Users demonstrate high engagement in both frequency and breadth, suggesting that depth (i.e., large investments in multiple categories) is a function of high frequency and diversified product usage.

Product Hook:

Goal: Increase conversion of users to add funds to their wallet after onboarding.

Success Metric: Increase in UPI and LRS transactions.

Problem Statement: There is a significant drop in users who abandon the platform before adding funds to their wallet or completing an LRS transaction to make their first investment, despite having completed the entire onboarding process.

Current Alternatives: After onboarding, users are directed to the dashboard, where they see a banner prompting them to add funds to their wallet in order to make their first investment.

Solution:
To improve conversion, users will be nudged immediately after completing the onboarding process to add funds to their wallet and complete LRS. A reward (cashback) will be offered for completing these actions on the same day, motivating users to finalize the process.

User Flow:

  • After onboarding, a pop-up appears with the message: "Add funds to your wallet to make your first investment" along with the cashback reward offer for completing the action on the same day.
  • When the user engages with the pop-up, they are directed to the "Add Funds" page to complete the transaction.

This approach streamlines the process and incentivizes users to take immediate action, improving conversion rates and reducing drop-off.

Retention Curve:


Retention Comparison: Other Competitors ( IndMoney, StockAl ) vs Appreciate

At this stage, comparing Appreciate with other competitors in the market may not be a fair assessment, as Appreciate is still in the early stages of scaling. However, if we evaluate retention across different segments, it could look like this:

  • Customer Support: IndMoney or other competitors offers strong customer support which positively impacts user retention.
  • Onboarding Process: Appreciate currently has a long onboarding process and challenges particularly with KYC verification, which create delays and make it harder for users to get started.
  • Referral Programs: Competitors like IndMoney have more effective referral programs, offering better incentives for users to invite others. This boosts retention by leveraging network effects.
