What is Liquide?

Investing can be overwhelming, especially for retail investors who lack expert guidance. That’s where Liquide comes in. It's a SEBI-registered investment advisory platform that simplifies stock market and mutual fund investing with AI-driven insights, expert trade recommendations, and real-time portfolio health checks. Whether you're a beginner or an experienced investor, the Liquide platform helps you make informed, confident decisions—backed by data, not guesswork. With over 3.5 million users and $2 billion in assets analyzed, Liquide redefines how Indians invest.

Best Engagement Framework for Liquide


As per the Core Value Proposition and Nature of the Product - Depth is the Best Engagement Framework that will drive overall retention.

Why Depth?

Depth measures how deeply a user engages with the platform’s core value proposition, rather than just how often they visit (Frequency) or how many features they use (Breadth).

Liquide’s core value is providing AI-powered, expert-backed investment insights to help users make smarter financial decisions. Now an Active user is someone who is not just checking AI recommendations but also taking an investment action. The more investment actions that an active user takes, the better the value proposition is realized.


Depth = { Frequency X Time/Money }


Depth for Liquide = 20 Investment actions X Rs. 10000 (Average Investment size)


Why not Frequency?

Users don’t need to log in daily (Frequency) to see value—but they must engage meaningfully with investment insights and act on them. If frequency is pushed as an engagement, it can increase churn users as high frequency relates to SPAM.

Why not Breadth or New Features?

The New Features or the sub-products like Screener, Wealth Baskets or News will not help directly in the core value proposition. These are more of a good Product hooks that can be used to increase Top of the mind awareness (TOMA)





Why does Behavioral Segmentation (Casual, Core, Power Users) Work Best?

As discussed, Depth is the best Framework for Liquide's Engagement. Our goal is to move users up the engagement ladder by building TOMA (Top of Mind Awareness)

  1. Convert Casual Users → Core Users (Engagement Focus).
  2. Keep Core Users → Power Users (Retention Focus).
  3. Retain Power Users


1️⃣ Casual User (Low Engagement, High Drop-off Risk)



  • Behavioral Traits:
    • New or infrequent users who haven’t fully experienced Liquide’s core value.
    • Tend to consume free insights or AI recommendations but rarely act on them.
    • May take occasional investment recommendations but haven’t fully integrated (e.g., broker account not connected).
    • Log in occasionally but don’t utilize deeper features like AI recommendations or trade alerts.
  • Barriers & Pain Points:
    • Trust Issues: Skeptical about the reliability of AI-based recommendations.
    • Lack of Urgency: View investing as a non-immediate action.
    • Overwhelmed by Complexity: Too many technical terms or steps confuse them; they need simplified, guided experiences.

2️⃣ Core Users (Engaged, Retaining, But Not Deeply Invested)


  • Behavioral Traits:
    • Regular users who have experienced Liquide’s value multiple times.
    • Have connected brokerage accounts and used AI recommendations for investments.
    • However, they haven’t fully explored or maximized features like News, Wealth Baskets, or Portfolio Insights.
    • Log in frequently (weekly or monthly) mainly to check portfolio updates.
  • Barriers & Pain Points:
    • Limited Utilization: Use core features but haven’t explored all offerings.
    • Need for Confidence: Seek more validation from AI recommendations before making larger investment decisions.
    • Desire for Deeper Insights: Want advanced analytics to stay engaged and optimize portfolios.

3️⃣ Power Users (Highly Engaged, High Lifetime Value)

  • Behavioral Traits:
    • Heavy users who actively integrate Liquide into their investment strategies.
    • Trust AI-driven insights and regularly act upon them.
    • Likely to refer friends, renew premium services, and contribute to Liquide's revenue growth.
    • Engage with multiple sub-products like mutual funds, screeners, and wealth baskets.
  • Barriers & Pain Points:
    • Customization Needs: Prefer more tailored and personalized AI recommendations.
    • Market Sensitivity: High market volatility could influence their engagement and trust levels.

When & Why Will the Retention Curve Flatten?


🔹 When?

🔹 The retention curve will flatten around 3-4 months—this is when users either:
✅ Find long-term value (using Liquide’s tools regularly) → They stay.
❌ Lose interest (if they don't see immediate returns or get overwhelmed) → They churn.


🔹 Why?

  • Investment platforms naturally have a lower long-term retention than entertainment/social apps because users don’t need daily engagement.
