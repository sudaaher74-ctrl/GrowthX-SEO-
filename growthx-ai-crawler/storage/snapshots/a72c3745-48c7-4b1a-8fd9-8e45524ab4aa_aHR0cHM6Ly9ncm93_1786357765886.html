What is FinAtoZ?

​

FinAtoZ is a financial advisory company based in Bangalore, India. They are a SEBI-registered investment advisor company [SEBI is the regulatory body for securities markets in India]

​

​

Here's a summary of what FinAtoZ does:

  • Provides unbiased financial advice to busy working professionals
  • Creates financial plans to help you reach your long-term goals, like retirement or your child's education
  • Uses a scientific approach to assess your risk tolerance to create a suitable investment portfolio
  • Recommends a variety of investment products including mutual funds, PMS, and peer-to-peer lending
  • Offers an online platform to track your investments and communicate with your advisor

​

What problem FinAtoZ is solving?

​

Busy working professionals often don't have the time or knowledge to properly manage their finances. FinAtoZ helps solve this problem by providing unbiased financial advice and creating financial plans to help them reach their long-term goals. They also use a scientific approach to assess risk tolerance and recommend suitable investment products.

​

FinAtoZ's core value proposition combines two key benefits:

  1. Achieving Financial Goals: They focus on helping busy professionals achieve their specific long-term goals, like retirement or child education, through personalized financial planning.
  2. Unbiased and Expert Guidance: They address the challenge of mis-selling in financial products by offering unbiased advice from SEBI certified professionals (CFPs, NISMs). This ensures you get recommendations based on your needs, not on what makes them the most money.

In essence, FinAtoZ offers peace of mind and a clear path towards achieving your financial goals through expert guidance you can trust.

The Indian Financial Advisory Market: A Flourishing Sector

​

The Financial Advisory Market in India has witnessed significant growth in recent years. A growing middle class with rising disposable income, coupled with increasing financial literacy, has fueled the demand for professional financial guidance. The Indian Financial Advisory Services Market was valued at approximately USD 1.3 billion in 2022 and is projected to reach USD 2.5 billion by 2028, indicating a promising CAGR of over 12%.

Learn about the Finance Market Outlook - Region- UAE, 2026F

Who are the Major Players? Top Financial Advisory Firms in India

The Indian Financial Advisory Sector boasts a diverse range of players, catering to various client segments. Here's a glimpse into the key categories:


  • Independent Financial Advisors (IFAs): These are individual advisors who provide personalized financial planning and investment advice, often specializing in specific areas like retirement planning or wealth management.
  • Registered Private Wealth Management Firms(RIAs): These cater to high-net-worth individuals (HNWIs) and offer comprehensive wealth management solutions, including portfolio management, estate planning, and tax optimization.
  • Robo-advisors: These are automated online platforms that utilize algorithms to provide investment management services, typically suited for low-cost, goal-based investing.

Market Sizing

​

We will be focusing on ICP1 - An IT employee earning more than 30 Lakhs per annum looking for holistic financial planning and investment advice seeker to fulfill his financial goals.

​

Deloitte & Nasscom Report: titled "Emerging technology hubs of India," acknowledges that a significant portion of the 5.4 million people in India's tech industry are clustered in seven major Tier 1 cities: Delhi, Mumbai, Kolkata, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, and Pune

​

​

Assumptions:

  1. Total IT Professionals in Tier 1 Cities: 5,400,000 (Source: NASSCOM)
  2. Income Threshold: ₹30 Lakhs
    • This is the minimum annual income level at which professionals might consider using a financial advisor.
  4. Financial Needs Proportion: 50%
    • This represents the percentage of IT professionals who have financial complexities and needs that make them likely to seek financial advisory services. These needs include managing investments, retirement planning, child education. This estimate accounts for a significant portion of high-income professionals who have varied and complex financial needs.
  6. Risk Tolerance Proportion: 40%
    • This represents the percentage of IT professionals who have a moderate risk tolerance. These individuals are more likely to seek balanced investment strategies that can provide steady growth without high risk. This estimate is based on industry studies indicating the distribution of risk tolerance among professionals.
  8. Investment Goals Proportion: 60%
    • This represents the percentage of IT professionals who prioritize long-term financial goals such as retirement planning and child education. These goals are common among high-income earners who are planning for future financial security and are likely to engage with financial advisors to achieve these goals.

Updated Calculations:

  1. TAM (Total Addressable Market):
    • TAM = Total IT Professionals × Financial Needs Proportion
    • Calculation: TAM = 5,400,000 × 0.5 = 2,700,000
  3. SAM (Serviceable Available Market):
    • SAM = Total IT Professionals × Risk Tolerance Proportion × Investment Goals Proportion
    • Calculation: SAM = 5,400,000 × 0.4 × 0.6 = 1,296,000
  5. SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market):
    • SOM = Total IT Professionals × Financial Needs Proportion × Risk Tolerance Proportion × Investment Goals Proportion
    • Calculation: SOM = 5,400,000 × 0.5 × 0.4 × 0.6 = 648,000
