About Perplexity

Perplexity is a free AI search engine designed to revolutionise the way you discover information. Ask any question, and it searches the internet to give you an accessible, conversational, and verifiable answer. Think of Perplexity as a research partner, always on the clock, saving you valuable time by providing the precise knowledge you need.

​

Core value Proposition - Advantages of Using Perplexity over Search Engines:

    1. Skip the links - Get a single, comprehensive answer that summarizes everything you need to know.
    2. Real-time information - Data is pulled from the internet the moment you ask a question, so information is always up-to-date.
    3. Reliable sources - Every answer is backed by citations from trusted news outlets, academic papers, and established blogs.
    4. File analysis - Upload pdf, csv documents and search within the files asking specific questions using chat interface.
    5. Perplexity Pro Search - Use it to summarise, solve complex problems with clarifying questions and interpret/ debug or generate code/ software programs.

Whether you are researching for professional projects, seeking answers to everyday questions, or exploring new topics to broaden your understanding, Perplexity is your definitive source for information.


Perplexity has 10 Lakh casual users in India. As per user research, majority of the users use it on free version and haven't showed any interest to take a Paid subscription which actually helps them unlock the true might of the product.

When compare other competitors (Google/ ChatGPT), Perplexity is in PMF stage in the Indian market.

​Let's understand Search & Competition:

People / Users use Search engines in various use cases and can be broadly categorized into 5 categories:


Explain me these search intents.
  1. Informational search intent - Intent of the user to locate a particular information snippet that satisfies a knowledge need that they are struggling with. There is a recognized need, with no clear solution. 
  2. Navigational search intent - User already knows the brand and they’re trying to locate something related to it on the web.
  3. Transactional search intent - Intent of the user demonstrates a desire to obtain something other than information which is typically a web-mediated transaction.
  4. Commercial search intent - User wants to complete a web-mediated transaction and they are already, for instance, brand-aware of one or two organizations in the space, but they are also seeking information about these brands.
  5. Localized search intent and other niche segments - User is seeking information about the brand, for instance – whether they have a shop that is offline, how they can contact the team.

​

What stage Perplexity is in?

Though Perplexity have 10 Lakh users in india, users are predominately casual.

Casual users are experiencing the core value proposition and pro features with a limit of 5 searches per day.


Though users are experiencing core value proposition of AI assisted search, with rationing of searches (5 Pro searches limit), customer is not experiencing the success of his/ her task at hand.

​

As per my user research, users are rarely using the product and doesn't want to upgrade to the paid service where they can truly experience the value of AI assisted search.

​

To further strengthen the product for the needs of Indian users, we are considering Perplexity Pro membership is in PMF stage. As there is some level of Product Market fit with Casual users, the goal is to find out potential power users and figure out PMF for Pro version of the product.


Perplexity is in between PMF stage and early stage. There are 10 Lakh casual users of the product in India but as per user research there is no clear evidence that users are deriving value out of it. ICPs are not able to use the full blown version of the product because of paywall. Because it's a software product, it won't cost company a lot to offer free trails to some of the core ICPs to observe the behaviour and usage of potential power users.

​

Jobs to be done at this stage:

  • Find Micro communities of potential power users and offer them free trails to observe and to estimate the value generated for the user on the product.
  • Create a habit of using perplexity with a core Business owners/ Working Professional ICP so that they experience success and integrates the product into their routine.
    (Offer 3 months/ 1 year free trail, adopting to new search interface takes users a long time)
    (Users already aware and perceive there is value in using AI assisted search because of PR and Media around them, but user is unsure how it will help him/ her personally as he haven't seen a real use case.)
