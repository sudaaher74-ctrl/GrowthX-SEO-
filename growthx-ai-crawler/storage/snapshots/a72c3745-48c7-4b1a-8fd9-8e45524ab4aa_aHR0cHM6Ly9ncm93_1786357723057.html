Product Recording


KamayaKya is a wealth management service startup founded in 2022 by Aniket Kulkarni and Dhiren Shah along with directors Nitya Shah and Anand Shah. The web app gives buy/sell recommendations to its users on Mainboard and SME board of Indian Stock Market. The team has a cumulative experience of over 50 years in equity markets and current have over 2000 investors showing strong PMF signs among the power users.

​

Customer Journey

​


As it is visible The investors take various external touchpoint in consideration to drive maximum conviction on the particular stock to decide whether to invest or not and how much to invest in. External touchpoint like:

  1. News resources: Online news websites, news apps, TV channels, etc.
  2. Screener tools: Screener.in, Trendlyne, Tickertape.
  3. Community: WhatsApp, ValuePickr, Telegram.


Litmus Test

  • Is there a PMF --> Yes. Strong satisfaction and WOM spread from existing power users.
  • Looking to drive power usage --> We need to increase Free to Paid user conversion.
  • Is there any customer request? --> Yes. Customer's have requested to receive news updates on our stock picks to make it easier for them to conduct research and keep engaged with the stocks
  • Difficult to acquire customer? --> Yes. We are a bootstrapped startup and are looking to acquire users with minimal to no CAC and run on a revenue share model.

First Call Design

Before the call

  1. Partner Current Goals- To have more paid users
  2. Justify reasoning-
    1. Partner needs more users with high appetite to pay for their yearly subscription of 21,000. KamayaKya's core ICP falls in that category and compliments our expertise and competency.
    2. Stock analytics is a high frequency use case which will drive more users to our platform and vice versa.
    3. Trendlyne aims to go global hence will attract NRI users for KamayaKya
  4. Value prop design- Get access to 5000 and growing passionate investor base at KamayaKya with high appetite to pay and equity investments. On the other hand Trendlyne user's shall get discounted access to KamayaKya thereby achieving great returns and hence high retention rate for Trednlyne.
