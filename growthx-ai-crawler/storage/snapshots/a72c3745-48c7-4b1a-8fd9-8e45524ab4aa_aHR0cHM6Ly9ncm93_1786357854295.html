Market Research - TAM, SAM and SOM


Total Addressable Market (TAM) - Data and Assumptions
  • Refyne targets salaried employees and potentially even gig workers who earn regular income and might need early access to wages.
  • According Economic Survey 2024, regular wage/salaried employees in India accounts for around 20.9% of total workforce which is 56.6Cr. Hence total salaried employees in India is 11.8Cr.
  • The report also mentions that gig workers for 1.5% of total workforce. Hence, gig workers make another 0.8Cr of regular wage/salaried employees in India.
  • With Refyne's potential to expand to gig workers and the informal sector, the total workforce (including gig workers) in India is (11.8 + 0.8) = 12.6Cr employees.
  • Hence the TAM for refyne in 2024 would be 12.6Cr.
Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM) - Data and Assumptions
  • Not all employees would need to access their wages early. Let’s assume 30% of salaried and gig workers might have a consistent need for early access due to financial stress, emergencies, etc.
  • Therefore, the potential user base could be 12.6Cr x 30% = 3.78Cr potential users.


Serviceable obtainable Market (SOM) - Data and Assumptions
  • Refyne holds a significant portion of the Indian Earned Wage Access (EWA) market. Since its launch in 2020, it has positioned itself as India's first and largest EWA provider, catering to over 1 million employees across more than 200 organizations.
  • Although there is growing competition due to emerging players in this space. But due to Refyne's broad reach and early-mover advantage its safe to assume that Refyne holds a market share of 35% - 40% in 2024.
  • Hence, Refyne's SOM would be 35 - 40% of SAM = 1.35Cr - 1.5Cr.



Core Value Proposition

The core value proposition of Refyne revolves around providing financial flexibility to employees through Earned Wage Access (EWA), which allows them to access their earned salary before payday. This addresses several pain points for employees and businesses:

  1. Financial Flexibility for Employees:
    Refyne helps employees avoid financial stress and dependence on high-interest loans by offering on-demand access to wages they've already earned. This reduces their reliance on loan sharks or expensive payday loans during financial emergencies​
  2. Increased Employee Well-being:
    By ensuring employees have access to wages when they need them, Refyne promotes financial stability, which is linked to better mental health, increased productivity, and improved overall satisfaction at work​
  3. Cost-Free for Employers:
    Refyne offers a no-cost integration with employers' existing payroll systems, enabling companies to offer a powerful financial benefit without incurring extra costs. This also helps employers reduce turnover and increase engagement as financial stress is a leading cause of job dissatisfaction​
  4. Simple, Transparent Fee Structure:
    For employees, Refyne operates on a simple transaction fee per use, with no complex financial structures or hidden charges. This transparency builds trust with users who know exactly what they're paying for access​

By addressing both employee financial well-being and employer needs in a simple, scalable model, Refyne is positioned as a market leader in India's EWA space.




Experiments to identify where existing acquisition strategy is optimized

Refyne's main acquisition channels currently leveraged, are

  • direct partnerships with companies and
  • integrations with HR platforms.

Refyne partners with over 150 companies, helping them integrate Earned Wage Access (EWA) solutions seamlessly without requiring substantial internal resources. However, given the high growth target (3 million employees by next year), optimizing acquisition channels that emphasize digital outreach, partnership expansion, and app-based user engagement could prove beneficial​.


Experiment 1: Organic Content loops optimization Optimization
  • Experiment: Develop UGC through testimonials (by end user how quick and easy it was to EWA using Refyne) and case studies (by HR managers about ease of use and retention numbers after implementing Refyne) for video-based content and publish on platforms like youtube or Instagram Reels, as video UGC is increasingly favored by algorithms.
  • White space: At the moment there is not much UGC on Instagram. However, there is some UGC on Youtube. Refyne could optimize that by creating both long and short video content and repurpose the same by trimming it down as reels for Instagram. In this way, they can get traffic from Instagram to Youtube and their website and app. These content could be used to target younger generation demographic interested in ICP.


Experiment 2: Paid Ads Optimization
  • Targeted LinkedIn Ads for Employer Acquisition: Run LinkedIn ads targeting HR leaders, CFOs, and operations managers within mid-to-large companies. Create separate ad variations based on job titles, company size, and industry (such as healthcare, retail, or manufacturing) to determine which segment responds best. Highlight Refyne’s unique value propositions like improved employee retention and no integration costs.
  • White Space: LinkedIn message ads or in-mail campaigns could provide a personalized approach to connect directly with decision-makers. Testing lead-gen forms integrated within ads, allowing users to submit their details without leaving LinkedIn, could also lower friction and boost conversion rates.


Experiment 3: Referral program Optimization
  • HR and Payroll Manager Referral Incentive Program : Develop a referral program specifically for HR and payroll managers who advocate for Refyne within their networks. Offer rewards such as discounts on service fees or free add-ons for successfully referring other organizations. Experiment with case study content that referrers can share to explain Refyne’s benefits more easily.
  • White Space : Adding a co-marketing opportunity for referrers, where they can showcase their company’s success with Refyne on social media or in webinars, could drive engagement. An exclusive online community for referrers to network, exchange ideas, and gain insights on EWA could further increase program adoption.

Refyne and Zoho Product Integration

Define Objectives and Success Metrics

  • Objective: Enable Darwinbox users (particularly HR and payroll managers) to seamlessly implement Refyne’s Earned Wage Access (EWA) within their existing HRMS and payroll workflows, making EWA accessible directly within the Darwinbox interface.

Success Metrics:

    • Adoption rate among Darwinbox’s existing and new clients.
    • Increase in employee engagement and satisfaction with payroll features.
    • Revenue growth from Darwinbox-integrated Refyne users.

2. Map Out Technical Requirements

  • API Integration:
    • Use Refyne’s API to connect with Darwinbox People (HR management) and Darwinbox Payroll, allowing real-time data synchronization of employee attendance, payroll, and leave details.
    • Ensure Refyne’s API can read payroll and work-hour data from Darwinbox systems to calculate eligible earned wages dynamically.
  • Data Security:
    • Ensure data encryption, compliance with industry standards for protecting sensitive employee and payroll data.
    • Implement secure token-based authentication for data access and sharing between Refyne and Darwinbox.
  • Synchronization Cadence:
    • Schedule data syncs to update payroll information in real-time or near-real-time, with fallback to batch processing for large datasets.

3. User Experience (UX) and Interface Design

  • Embedded Refyne Features within Darwinbox:
    • Add an “Earned Wage Access” tab in Darwinbox People and Darwinbox Payroll, allowing employees to view and access earned wages directly within their HR portal.
    • Integrate a Refyne dashboard for HR managers within Darwinbox, providing data on EWA usage, payout history, and engagement metrics.
  • Employee Self-Service:
    • Enable employees to view and request advances directly from their Darwinbox accounts.
    • Offer real-time tracking of available earned wages, past withdrawals, and notifications.

4. Measure Outcomes and Optimize

  • Track the impact of the integration on HR satisfaction, employee usage rates, and client retention.
  • Identify features to enhance based on adoption data, such as automating additional workflows or refining the employee self-service interface.
