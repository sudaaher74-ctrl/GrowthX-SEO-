Acquisition project | Fino Club

Fino Club is an exclusive, high-value community for traders, analysts, and financial enthusiasts. The primary goal is to provide a platform where members can learn, discuss, and share insights about the financial markets, including stocks, crypto, FX, and trade ideas.

Problem

  • ​Lack of Quality Education and Resources:
    • Many aspiring traders and investors struggle to find reliable and high-quality educational resources.
    • Fino Club provides structured, comprehensive courses and mentorship programs tailored to various skill levels.

  • Limited Access to Expert Insights:
    • Retail traders often lack access to the insights and strategies used by professional traders.
    • Fino Club offers access to exclusive content and strategies shared by top traders and financial experts.

  • Isolation and Lack of Networking:
    • Trading can be a solitary activity, making it difficult for individuals to share ideas and get feedback.
    • Fino Club creates a community where members can network, share insights, and collaborate.

  • Information Overload:
    • The financial markets are inundated with information, making it hard to discern valuable insights.
    • Fino Club curates high-quality, relevant content, providing members with the most important and actionable information.

Customer benefits

  • Exclusive Community Access:
    • Members gain access to an invite-only community of top traders, analysts, and financial enthusiasts.

  • High-Quality Educational Content:
    • Comprehensive courses, webinars, and workshops covering various aspects of trading and investing.

  • Expert Insights and Strategies:
    • Access to trading strategies and insights from industry professionals and successful traders.

  • Networking Opportunities:
    • Regular events, meetups, and online forums where members can network, share ideas, and collaborate.

  • Practical Tools and Resources:
    • Access to trading tools, market analysis reports, and other resources to aid in trading decisions.

Understanding the Users

  1. Engaging with Active Members

I started by connecting with our current members. These are the individuals who live and breathe Fino Club every day- and have been supportive from the ideation stage. Through one-on-one interviews and group discussions, I learned what they love about the platform—the sense of community, the exclusive insights, and the opportunity to learn from the best in the field [ and eagerly waiting for programs to launch ] . But I also heard about their frustrations: the occasional feeling of being overwhelmed by the sheer volume of content, or the desire for more personalized trading advice. By listening, I was able to pinpoint key areas for improvement and new opportunities to enhance their experience. While reducing the number of channels and content which was less impactful.

  1. Potential Members

Next, I turned my attention to those who aren’t yet part of our community but should be. These are the ambitious traders and financial enthusiasts who could benefit immensely from Fino Club but haven’t taken the plunge { they know about us through free community and social Media }. I spoke with them directly, understanding their hesitation—whether it’s a lack of awareness or simply the challenge of breaking away from their current networks. This feedback was crucial in refining our outreach strategy, making it more targeted and compelling and we need to make our new website launch live ASAP ~ only community and beta forms have already top lined.

  1. Learning from Churned Members

While being a new brand we have v low churn .I wanted to have an Understanding why people leave is just as important as knowing why they stay. I reached out to former members, those who had once been engaged but drifted away. These conversations were eye-opening. Some had found value elsewhere, while others felt their needs had evolved beyond what Fino Club currently offers. This taught me that we need to not only continually innovate but also ensure we’re evolving alongside our members' growing sophistication and needs.Also I understood about the lifespan of users on market - Being a key reason for the churn.

  1. Analyzing Competitor Users

To truly differentiate Fino Club, I needed to understand what makes our competitors tick. I engaged with users of other financial communities, finding out what drew them there and what kept them engaged. This competitive analysis revealed both our strengths and areas where we could do better was less as per current information ~ but there are lot of places which we can cut down on. Armed with this knowledge, I worked on sharpening our unique value proposition, ensuring Fino Club stands out in a crowded market.

5. Mapping the Stakeholder Ecosystem

Finally, I mapped out the broader ecosystem—identifying decision-makers, influencers, and even blockers. By understanding the power dynamics and the key players within the financial community, I was able to develop a strategic plan to win over these critical stakeholders specifically , parents and peers of different age groups. Whether it’s through exclusive partnerships, influencer collaborations, or simply better communication of our value, I ensured Fino Club was positioned to succeed on all fronts.

1. Total Addressable Market (TAM):

  • Regions: Dubai, Singapore, and India
  • Total Potential Customers: 3,800,000 (adjusted for more conservative estimates)
  • ARPU: ₹15,000
  • TAM = ₹57,000 Crores

2. Serviceable Available Market (SAM):

Target Market Segment:

  • Reasoning: We’ll target a smaller, more precise segment of financially capable and engaged users, based on more conservative assumptions:
    1. India: We'll now focus on 15% of the potential market, recognizing that financial literacy and willingness to invest in such platforms are still growing.
    2. Dubai and Singapore: We'll adjust to 30% of the market, considering the smaller but affluent populations.

SAM Calculation:

  • India: 3,000,000 x 15% = 450,000 customers
  • Dubai: 500,000 x 30% = 150,000 customers
  • Singapore: 300,000 x 30% = 90,000 customers
  • Total SAM Customers: 450,000 + 150,000 + 90,000 = 690,000 customers
  • SAM Revenue: 690,000 x ₹15,000 = ₹10,350,000,000 (₹10,350 Crores)

3. Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM):

Market Penetration/Share:

  • Reasoning: We’ll use a more conservative market penetration estimate, recognizing that building market share takes time, especially in competitive spaces:
    1. India: Targeting 5% market penetration, considering the large, competitive landscape and the need for brand building.
    2. Dubai and Singapore: Targeting 7% market penetration, given higher levels of financial activity but still considering competition and market maturity.

SOM Calculation:

  • India: 450,000 x 5% = 22,500 customers
  • Dubai: 150,000 x 7% = 10,500 customers
  • Singapore: 90,000 x 7% = 6,300 customers
  • Total SOM Customers: 22,500 + 10,500 + 6,300 = 39,300 customers
  • SOM Revenue: 39,300 x ₹15,000 = ₹589,500,000 (₹589.5 Crores)

Detailing our Acquisition Channel

​Organic Channel

Current Strategy & Plan :

  • Success: Founders of Fino Club has successfully leveraged content around exclusive trading insights and market analysis, generating organic traffic from finance-focused communities and social media groups.
  • Failure: Lack of SEO optimization on educational content, leading to missed opportunities in search visibility. Additionally, not enough diverse content formats, such as video and interactive tools.

    Next Steps planned :

1: Keyword Research

    • Tools Used: Google Keyword Planner, Ahrefs [ Main ], SEMrush.
    • Keywords Identified: “trading community,” “finance mentorship,” “crypto trading strategies,” “forex market tips,” “stock trading education.”
      • Opportunities: High search volume and low competition in niche finance topics like “global speculation markets” and “private trading clubs.”Also lookiong for niche traffic from long tail words

2: Collated Insights

    • Search Intent: Users are looking for exclusive insights, beginner trading education, and market predictions.
    • Content Gap: Competitors are not fully addressing global market perspectives or offering real-time market analysis for niche audiences.

New Strategies currently in play: // Will start the SEO and few ther levers post new website completion//

    • SEO Focus: Optimize existing content with targeted keywords, ensuring meta descriptions, headers, and alt texts are fully optimized.
    • Content Expansion: Introduce video tutorials on YouTube, infographics, and webinars. Start a podcast featuring top traders from Fino Club.
    • Platform Diversification: Expand organic efforts to Quora by answering finance-related questions and linking back to Fino Club, and to LinkedIn with finance-focused articles.

Content Loop

1: Content Creators, Distributors, and Channels

    • Creators: Leveraging Fino Club’s in-house experts, top traders within the community, and guest experts from the finance industry as we scale and make name for ourselves.
    • Distributors: Main distribution channels will be LinkedIn, Twitter being the most important as the finance community hangout, YouTube, and the Fino Club website.
    • Channels: Focus on LinkedIn for professional content, YouTube for educational videos, and Medium for in-depth articles.

2: Loop Type

Evergreen content loop, where each piece of content continues to draw traffic over time, with occasional viral content focused on trending market events.
Regularly updating blog posts with new insights and data, creating a loop where users return for the latest information.

Step 3: Create a Flow Diagram

  • Flow Diagram: Content Creation → Distribution on Social Media & Website → User Engagement & Feedback → Content Optimization & New Content Creation → Repeat.
  • Example: A trading tip posted on LinkedIn links back to a detailed article on Fino Club, which is shared on social media, generating discussions and driving traffic back to the platform.

Paid Ads

CAC: LTV Ratio

  • Current Ratio: After calculating, Fino Club’s CACratio is found to be 1:4, indicating that paid ads are a viable growth option. // Used previous business data to get this output //

Digital Channels

    • Channels Chosen: Google Ads, Facebook Ads, Instagram Ads, YouTube Ads.
    • Rationale: Google Ads for search intent-driven traffic, Facebook and Instagram Ads for broader awareness and engagement, and YouTube Ads for visual content engagement. We will be scaling ad platfroms and channels as we grow - sticking to 1 for the initial time.

Audience Selection & Creative Strategy

    • Audience: Segmented into beginners (interested in trading education), intermediate traders (seeking mentorship), and advanced traders (looking for exclusive insights).
    • Creative Strategy: Use testimonials from top traders in the community , showcase success stories , and highlight the exclusive nature of Fino Club. Create video ads that illustrate the real-time trading environment and community benefits.

Ad Campaign

    • Ad Formats: A/B testing of video ads and carousel ads, focusing on high-performing creatives.
    • Campaign Objective: Driving traffic to a landing page offering a sneak peek into the Fino Club community.

Ad Budget

    • Initial Budget: Allocate 70% to high-performing channels (e.g., Google and IG and 30% to testing newer platforms or strategies.
