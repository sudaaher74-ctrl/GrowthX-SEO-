Absolutely not. We don't make a single penny off these offsites, quite the opposite. We've done this three times before, and each time, we've actually spent 10 lakh in 2022, 5 lakh in 2023, 9 lakhs in 2024 & 7.5L again in 2025 and this year we will be putting 7.5 lakh rupees just to make sure you all have an unforgettable experience.

Community team puts in 60 days for making an event like this happen. From personally visiting venues to vendor negotiations and even securing licenses. It's a labor of love, and we believe it's worth every minute to provide you with an invaluable experience.
