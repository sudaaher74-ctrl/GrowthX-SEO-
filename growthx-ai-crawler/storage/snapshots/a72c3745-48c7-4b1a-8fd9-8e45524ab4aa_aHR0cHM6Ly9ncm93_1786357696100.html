• What does the Haqdarshak App solves for?

The Haqdarshak App solves for the following-

  1. Haqdarshak App provides information of over 80 government schemes including the eligibility criteria, documents required and benefits received.
  2. Haqdarshak App also provides assistance and guidance for scheme application through its trained agents.
  3. Haqdarshak's USP- The screening criteria of the Haqdarshak App helps an individual to all government schemes he/she is eligible by answering a few questions.


• Core Value Propositions-

  1. Pain Killers-

a) Convenience- Scheme Information is available at the fingertips.

b) Accessibility- Providing Scheme benefits to the marginalised and down trodden communities living in the most remote villages of the country.

c) Risk Reduction- Scheme application through the Haqdarshak App reduces the risk of rejection of scheme applications as the app enables the customer to apply for schemes which they are actually eligible for under proper guidance of the agents.

  1. Gain Creators-

a) Performance- The Haqdarshak App is an effective way of applying for government schemes as compared to applying individually as that might lead to application rejection due to several reasons.

b) Customisation- Our screening will help a customer know schemes that they are eligible for, which is unique to them.

c) Getting the Job Done- Post booking process on the Haqdarshak App helps a customer to meet the agent to discuss which documents are required and which schemes to apply for.

CALCULATE TAM-


I have taken Top Down Approach to calculate TAM for Haqdarshak App. Following is the order that I will follow to calculate the same-

  1. Total number of smartphone users in India= 1 billion
  2. Total number of android smartphone users in India (95%)= 950000000
  3. Government makes Schemes for = 850 million indians
  4. Population of prioritised ICPs = 586 million workers and MSMEs.
  5. Population % of ICPs which own android phones = 94.95%


TAM for Haqdarshak App= 556,407,000


CALCULATE SAM-


To calculate the Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM) for Haqdarshak App, we need to consider factors such as the target audience for Haqdarshak's services and the extent to which it can effectively reach and serve this audience.


Given:

- Total number of workers and MSMEs (ICPs) = 586 million

- Percentage of ICPs who own Android smartphones ≈ 94.95%


SAM refers to the portion of the TAM that Haqdarshak can realistically target and serve with its services. In this context, we can assume that Haqdarshak aims to reach all Informal and MSMEs workers and enterprises who own Android smartphones and can benefit from its assistance in accessing government welfare schemes and services.


Therefore, the SAM would be equal to the number of ICPs who own Android smartphones:


SAM = Number of ICPs with Android smartphones = 556,407,000


Therefore, the Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM) is approximately 556.1 million prioritized Informal and MSMEs workers and enterprises who own Android smartphones. This represents the portion of the Total Addressable Market (TAM) that Haqdarshak can effectively target and serve with its services.


CALCULATE SOM-


To calculate the Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM) based on the provided information, we need to consider the potential reach and adoption of Haqdarshak's services among the Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM).


We know that:

- Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM) = 556.1 million prioritized Informal and MSMEs workers and enterprises who own Android smartphones.


The Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM) represents the portion of the SAM that Haqdarshak can realistically acquire and serve within a certain period.


To estimate the SOM, we need to consider factors such as market penetration, adoption rates, competition, and Haqdarshak's capacity to scale its operations.


Let's assume a hypothetical SOM adoption rate of 20%:


SOM = SAM * Adoption Rate

SOM = 556.1 million * 20%

SOM = 111.22 million


Therefore, the Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM) based on the provided information and the assumed adoption rate is approximately 111.22 million prioritized Informal and MSMEs workers and enterprises who own Android smartphones. This represents the portion of the Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM) that Haqdarshak can realistically acquire and serve within the defined parameters.

  1. Content Loops-


• Scheme Information- Haqdarshak has a repository of all government schemes and they are constantly updating their knowledge base. This content can be shared with over 850 million indians across several social media platforms, website, etc.

Following are the 3 experiments to acquire users from content loop channel-

  1. Educational Content Loop:

- Haqdarshak creates infographics, videos, and blog posts explaining various government welfare schemes and entitlements on Linkedin and Instagram.

- People access this content on Haqdarshak's website or social media channels (Instagram, LinkedIn, etc) and find it informative.

- People share the content on their own social media accounts, spreading awareness about available welfare schemes.

- Haqdarshak monitors social media for user-generated content related to their posts and reshare relevant user-generated content on their own social media accounts, acknowledging and encouraging user participation

  1. Success Stories Content Loop:

- Haqdarshak shares success stories of individuals who have successfully accessed government schemes and benefits through their assistance.

- Followers and beneficiaries engage with these stories, finding them inspiring and relatable.

- Some individuals who have benefited from Haqdarshak's services share their own success stories on social media, tagging Haqdarshak.

- Haqdarshak identifies and reshare these user-generated success stories, highlighting the impact of their work and motivating others to seek assistance.

  1. Interactive Q&A Content Loop:

- Haqdarshak conducts live Q&A sessions or posts interactive quizzes related to government schemes and entitlements.

- Followers engage with these posts, asking questions and participating in quizzes to test their knowledge.

- Some followers share screenshots of the Q&A sessions or quiz results on their social media accounts, inviting their friends and followers to join.

- Haqdarshak acknowledges user participation by resharing select screenshots or quiz results on their own social media platforms, fostering a sense of community and learning.


These content loops leverage user engagement and participation to amplify Haqdarshak's reach and impact, while also encouraging individuals to become more informed about available government welfare schemes and services.

4. Referrals-

  1. Bragworthy feature: The brag-worthy aspect of Haqdarshak lies in its transformative impact on people's lives. Customers continually praise Haqdarshak for its unwavering commitment to empowering individuals and communities through access to government welfare schemes and entitlements.
  2. Platform Currency: The platform currency of Haqdarshak App is the value it provides to its users by offering information, guidance, and support in accessing government welfare schemes and services. Users engage with the platform to avail themselves of these services and resources, enhancing their overall well-being and socio-economic status..
  3. Who should be asked for Referrals: Referrals should be asked from those Haqdarshak App users who have experienced the service delivered to them by a Haqdarshak agent in at least one scheme application, the benefit of which has been received by the user.
  4. Referral Discovery: Referrals should be discovered via- In-app banners, personalized referral messages, social media prompts, emails, SMSs.
  5. Sharing mechanism of Referrals- Referral codes can be considered as the sharing mechanism for Haqdarshak App.
    The communication would say- 'Let your friends and family know about Haqdarshak'.
    This code can be copied from the Haqdarshak App and can be pasted to WhatsApp or other social media messengers which the referred person can use to download the Haqdarshak App from his phone.
  6. The referrals can be tracked by the referees at the referral code page itself. Referral Page attached below-
  7. The referral invite for a non-user would have - "You have been invited" written at the top. Followed by the copy-
    "Your friend <Referee Name> gave you free screening of government schemes on Haqdarshak App. Download the Haqdarshak App to unlock scheme benefits"
    This can be followed by a CTA of "Download Haqdarshak App".
  8. The referees can be incentivised by 1 free service delivery of scheme application by a Haqdarshak Agent, every time a referral code has been used to download the Haqdarshak App.
