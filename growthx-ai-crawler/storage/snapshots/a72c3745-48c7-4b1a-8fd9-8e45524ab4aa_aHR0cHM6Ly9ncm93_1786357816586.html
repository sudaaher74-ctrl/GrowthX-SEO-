Acquisition Channel

Channel Strategy


Shopify grows its user base using various methods like paid channels, partnerships,and product integrations. Paid channels and SEO have been especially effectiv. However for Shopify to increase its presence in India, they need to put focus on partnership and market development activities and here is why


  • The cultural preference in India, where people may not be comfortable with a do-it-yourself approach, Shopify needs to provide more guidance and support to help users build their businesses. Source - Amazon partnership with Prione to enable small and medium businesses (SMBs) in India's fast-growing digital economy, Ikea Partnership with Urbanclap for furniture Instalment.


  • While launching an online is the first step the major is how to get the first "10 sales" for a brand to continue the journey and have a higher LTV and better ROI for shopify



Channels

Based on the strategy shared above and pain points shared by the ICP the two key channels for Shopify will be

  1. Market Development
  2. Partnership


  1. Market Development

With this approach Shopify will be partnering with business who also empowers entrepreneurs to start their online store in India. By partnering with these business Shopify can meet the prospective customers where they are increasing the conversion.

  • Educational Partners - Partnering up with education leaders like Digital Vidya, Shopify enhances user acquisition in India. Integrating practical Shopify applications into the curriculum showcases e-commerce capabilities to a diverse audience, boosting brand credibility. Exclusive incentives for participants opting for Shopify create a feedback loop, fostering a community that advocates for the platform. This aligns with Digital Vidya's educational goals, presenting Shopify as a practical solution for successful online stores.
  • Commerce Partner - Partnering with industry leaders like Meta, Google, Shiprocket, PayU, Razorpay and more for co-created events and webinars allows Shopify to tap into established audiences, position itself as a comprehensive e-commerce solution, and drive user acquisition in India. Collaborative efforts in education, cross-promotion, exclusive integrations, and community building contribute to Shopify's visibility and appeal among businesses seeking a seamless online store setup.
  • Product Partners - Partnering with GlowRoad, Roposo, and IndiaMART to integrate with Shopify streamlines the supplier base for dropshippers, significantly boosting user acquisition in India. This collaboration provides dropshippers with seamless access to a diverse range of suppliers and products, making Shopify the preferred e-commerce platform for efficient and scalable dropshipping businesses in the Indian market.


  1. Partner Program

The current Shopify Partner Program is predominantly classified in three buckets-

  • Agency Partners
  • Marketing Partners
  • App Partners


While Shopify has a large base of partners finding the quality partners who can acquire customers/brands in India are limited. Below are the few pressing points

  • Less hand holding on the learning and training
  • Lack of Recognition and Incentives:
  • Difficulty in Accessing New Features:
  • Complex Processes


To compact the above challenges Shopify can implement following solutions to increase the quality of partners to build a robust acquisition channel.

  1. Advanced Training and Certification Programs:
    • Offer specialized training and certification programs for partners to deepen their expertise in specific areas such as app development, theme design, or marketing strategies.
    • Provide advanced courses that cover the latest industry trends, technologies, and best practices.
  3. Early Access to Features and Beta Testing:
    • Grant partners early access to new features and updates, allowing them to test and integrate these advancements into their solutions before the general release.
    • Encourage partners to participate in beta testing programs, collecting valuable feedback and insights for continuous improvement.
  5. Enhanced Marketing and Co-Marketing Opportunities:
    • Offer co-marketing opportunities for partners, promoting their apps, services, or success stories through Shopify's marketing channels.
    • Provide tools and resources to help partners market their solutions effectively, including joint marketing campaigns and promotional materials.
  7. Community Events and Workshops:
    • Host regular virtual and in-person events, such as workshops, webinars, and conferences, where partners can learn from Shopify experts, share knowledge, and stay updated on the latest developments.
    • Facilitate hands-on training sessions and hackathons to encourage innovation within the partner community.
  9. Feedback Mechanisms:
    • Establish a structured feedback mechanism where partners can provide insights and suggestions for improving the Partner Program.
    • Actively incorporate partner feedback into decision-making processes to create a more collaborative and responsive program.
