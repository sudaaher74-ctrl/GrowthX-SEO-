Acquisition project | Vyapar Apps | Poorna

Changing the way Indian MSMEs do business

PRODUCT

Problem: MSMEs currently use pen and paper to manager all their billing, inventory, purchase, credit and party receivables/payments etc. This is leads inefficient management of the business and hinders growth and sometimes causes the business to shut down. Issues with paper billing

    1. Tiresome to store and fetch records.
    2. Almost impossible to track payments on time.
    3. No view of the stock status and trends in stocks
    4. Party payment reminders.
    5. Unable to bill quickly for FMCG or Kirana space.
    6. Compile sale and purchase bills every month/quarter to make GST compliances - Tedious task/Heavy cost involved (payments to CA).
    7. Unable to grow business digitally - say create a domain or online store to share items for purchase.
    8. Track expenses of the business and do optimizations wherever necessary.
    9. No visibility of price and discounts for the customers.
    10. Cannot create a brand and printer heavy etc.

​


Product Description: Vyapar is a Billing and Accounting Software designed for MSMEs of India. Idea of the application is that the merchant can easily move his business from Pen and Paper to Completely Digital set up without help of any additional training on how to use the software.

​

Have listed core features of the product below.

  1. Create GST bills and multiple kinds of sale invoices formats.
  2. Manage Inventory - Built on FIFO model (First in First out), restricted usage when it comes to pharmacy, perishables and couple other industries.
  3. GST reports - Readymade GST reports to make GST compliances easy. (GST Filing and Annual ITR returns)
  4. Party payment reminders - Sending automated payment reminders to parties which have to make payments via WhatsApp.
  5. Quick billing - A POS model to make quick billing at retail stores (Kirana, Supermarket, and any small retail stores)

Create a utility of sorts in the existing calling dashboards for all the calling agents across teams to gather referrals (user mobile, name and email) from happy customers and drop them in the remarks while marking calling disposition.
