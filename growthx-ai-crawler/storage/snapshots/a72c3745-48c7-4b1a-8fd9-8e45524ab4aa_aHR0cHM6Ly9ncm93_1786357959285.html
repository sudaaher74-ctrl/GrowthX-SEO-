This CRAFT is made for Founders, Product Managers, Growth leads, Marketers or anyone looking to launch. Note: If you’re looking to find your first 1000 customers, this CRAFT is not for you.

This CRAFT will give you a framework nail any GTM. You start by defining the context and goal for your GTM strategy, then identify the key levers to achieve it. Segment your ideal customer profiles (ICPs) for maximum reach and prioritize the right channels. Prepare thorough channel and distribution plans, and learn to align and influence stakeholders along the way. Set clear milestones to track progress and use a solid framework to analyze success post-launch. Finally, master the art of debriefing stakeholders to build trust and ensure ongoing alignment.
