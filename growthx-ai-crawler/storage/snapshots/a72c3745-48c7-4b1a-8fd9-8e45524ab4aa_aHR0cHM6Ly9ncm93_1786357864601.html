What is Smallcase?

A smallcase is a basket or a portfolio of stocks or ETFs, that tracks a theme, strategy or objective. These modern investment products, that are created by professionals, help users build a low-cost, long-term & diversified portfolio easily.


  • Simple to understand 🔖
    smallcases are modern investing products based on simple ideas users can understand.
  • Curated by India's top investment managers 🧑‍🎓
    smallcases are created & managed by leading professionals who are SEBI registered research analysts and investment advisors
  • Designed for users 🙍‍♀️
    smallcases are fully customisable. Users can edit their smallcase constituents any time or create their own smallcase.


What does smallcase offer its users?

  • Buy multiple stocks in 1 click
    Place orders for up to 50 stocks in 1 click as a smallcase and save time
  • Track smallcases anytime, anywhere
    Track & manage seamlessly with a real time index value, inclusive of corp. actions & dividends
  • Invest regularly with SIPs
    Invest in a disciplined manner by starting a SIP on your smallcase for every week, month or quarter
  • Create your own smallcase
    Be your own fund manager & invest in your ideas by creating a smallcase of up to 50 stocks



Who are the Users?

Following 2 are the ICPs:

  1. The Busy Investor 👨‍💼🧳⌚
    This cohort of users wants to invest regularly and keep a track on their investment portfolio, but they are too busy and often forget about keeping a tab. They find investing into multiple stocks regularly mentally draining and time consuming. Does not have enough to see the stocks performing well or doing bad and hence doesn’t end up rebalancing the portfolio whenever needed. These users have good investment intent and high appetite to pay. They need convenient solutions for this problem.
  2. The Novice Investor 🧑 👩
    This cohort of users are beginner investors who have a will to invest but lack the knowledge and expertise. This group lacks time for research or monitoring of portfolio. They need a professional advice to plan their investments well, at the same time want flexible and high yielding investment instruments.

Activation Metrics

Here are a few Hypothesis for Activation Metrics:

Hypothesis 1: Connecting their demat/broker account in first 3 days

Reasoning: Connecting an account could be a big step because that shows trust in the platform and a will to do some action after connecting the account. This shows a high will to buy and alter smallcases in the future, and hence is a first sign of being retained on platform.


Hypothesis 2: Investing in 1 smallcase in 1st week of signup

Reasoning: The moment a user lands on the homescreen, they are shown “Ideal to get started” smallcases with the number of people buying them in this week. Users are also given “All Weather Investing” as a starter for new users. So researching and investing in the first smallcase should not be that tough given the nudges being done to new user. If the user invests in their first transaction as early as possible, he/she will go through the entire experience once and will understand the entire flow and features and will gain trust, which is the most important thing.


Hypothesis 3: Start an SIP in first month

Reasoning: If a user starts an SIP on the platform then they are highly likely to retain on the platform since SIPs are generally done over long term and most often auto-debit feature is on which ensures long term association with platform.


Hypothesis 4: Making a demat/broker account with smallcase in first 7 days

Reasoning: A user who doesn’t have any existing broker account and wants to create an account with smallcase shows high trust and high will to invest in smallcase. Having a broker account with smallcase (that too first) is a strong signal that they will buy atleast 1 smallcase and be a retained user on the platform.


Hypothesis 5: Diversify in 2 smallcases in 1 month

Reasoning: A user who has already invested in 1 smallcase which might be a small ticket size investment considering the “getting-started” smallcases have a low minimum amount of ~ Rs. 300. This user upon seeing the performance of first smallcase and scrolling on the app gets to know other similar smallcases with low minimum amount, can start investing in second smallcase. This shows that the user is engaged and trusts the brand by investing in different types of smallcases for diversification, and hence will be retained on the platform.


Hypothesis 6: Create your own smallcase in 1st month

Reasoning: When a user has put in efforts to create their own smallcase, which enabled them to create a personalised investment tool, they are much likely to be engaged and retained on the platform since they’ve created this smallcase by their research and they trust their judgement. Like the ikea effect. They needed a place to strategise and plan their investment systematically and crating your own smallcase lets you do that. So these users are utilizing the features well, putting their efforts and are trusting their judgement, hence they’ll retain with the platform in the longer run.


Hypothesis 7: Taking Top-ups on smallcase within 1st month

Reasoning: If a user who has already invested in a smallcase, wants to top-up their smallcase by putting more money, this shows that the user has found the platform useful and trustworthy to invest larger sum. This validates the user’s stickiness and the potential of them becoming a power user on the platform.


Hypothesis 8: Adding smallcases to watchlist in the first 2 days

Reasoning: Since smallcase is a relatively new form of investment, many people might not be aware of the functioning of this investment instrument. And when such a user comes to the platform and is made aware of the different types of smallcases available, it can be a little confusing for them to choose their first smallcase. In such a time when the user spends time on the platform and watchlist their most liked smallcases, this shows their engagement on the platform and are likely to convert that watchlist to investment. This is not a strong factor but could be tracked since they put in time to research.
