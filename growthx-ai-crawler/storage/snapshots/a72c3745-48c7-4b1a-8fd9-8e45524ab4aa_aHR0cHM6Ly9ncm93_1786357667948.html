About Event

​Celebrate India Together 🇮🇳

​Independence Day feels a little different when you're away from home.

​The familiar food, the languages you grew up hearing, the places you miss, and the small things that instantly remind you of India.

​This is a simple Independence Day gathering for Indians around the world to come together, meet new people, share stories, and celebrate the feeling of home wherever they are.

​Why is it happening?

​India is more than a place. It's the food we grew up eating, the languages we speak, the cities we come from, the traditions we carry, and the memories we take with us wherever we go.

​This Independence Day, we're bringing Indians in the city together to celebrate that shared connection.

​Just good conversations, Indian food, new people, old memories, and an evening that feels a little like home. 💙

​Who is this for?

​This is for Indians living away from home who want to meet others in the city and celebrate Independence Day together.

​Whether you've lived here for years, recently moved, are studying, building a company, working, creating, or figuring out!

​What Happens on the Day?

​We’ll get together over Indian food and spend the evening meeting people, sharing stories, and celebrating India together.

​Talk about the cities you grew up in, the things you miss about home, your favourite food, traditions, childhood memories, or simply what being Indian means to you today.
