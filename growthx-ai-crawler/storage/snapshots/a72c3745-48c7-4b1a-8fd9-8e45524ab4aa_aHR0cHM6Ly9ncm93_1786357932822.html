What Should Netflix Charge For?

Pricing LeverApplication to NetflixShould Netflix Charge?Rationale

Monthly/Annual plans (₹149–₹649)

✅ Yes (Primary model)

Charge per movie/show/episode (pay-per-view)

❌ No (For now)

✅ Yes

✅ Yes
