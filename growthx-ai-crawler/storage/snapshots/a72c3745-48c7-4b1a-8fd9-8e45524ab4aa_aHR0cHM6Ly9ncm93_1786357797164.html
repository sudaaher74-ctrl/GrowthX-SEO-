Tiered Partnership Program​

Bronze Partner

The entry-level tier for new or smaller partners. This level provides basic incentives and initial support to help partners get started.

Incentives:

  • Commission: 10% on Monthly Recurring Revenue (MRR) per user.
  • Training Access: Basic training resources and onboarding support.
  • Co-Marketing Activities:
    1. Access to RouteMagic branding materials and co-branded marketing templates.
    2. Listing as an official partner on RouteMagic’s website.

Requirements:

    1. Sales Volume: 0-49 users onboarded.
    2. Revenue Contribution: Up to £10,000 in annual MRR.
    3. Customer Retention Rates: N/A at this level.
    4. Training: Basic certification required.


​

Silver Partner
Partners who have shown moderate success, achieving specific sales thresholds and starting to build a significant customer base.

Incentives

  • Commission: 12% on MRR per user.
  • Training Access: Advanced training resources, including webinars and partner-exclusive content.
  • Co-Marketing Activities:
      1. Joint blog posts highlighting partner success stories.
      2. Inclusion in joint email campaigns targeting specific customer segments.
      3. Opportunity to be featured in RouteMagic’s social media channels.

​Requirements:

  1. Sales Volume: 50-99 users onboarded.
  2. Revenue Contribution: £10,001 - £50,000 in annual MRR.
  3. Customer Retention Rates: Minimum 75% annual retention rate.
  4. Training: Completion of Advanced Certification.


​

Gold Partner
High-performing partners who consistently deliver significant sales volumes and maintain strong customer relationships, with access to premium incentives and higher levels of support.

Incentives:

  • Commission: 15% on MRR per user.
  • Training Access: Full access to all training materials, including advanced certifications and direct support from RouteMagic’s product team.
  • Co-Marketing Activities:
      1. Participation in joint case studies to showcase successful implementations.
      2. Joint webinars or workshops targeted at potential customers.
      3. Priority inclusion in RouteMagic’s lead generation campaigns.
  • Requirements:
    1. Sales Volume: 100-199 users onboarded.
    2. Revenue Contribution: £50,001 - £200,000 in annual MRR.
    3. Customer Retention Rates: Minimum 80% annual retention rate.
    4. Training: Master Certification required.


​

Platinum Partner
The elite tier for partners who excel in all aspects of their partnership with RouteMagic, including top-tier sales, customer retention, and overall contribution. Platinum partners receive exclusive benefits and the highest incentives.

Incentives:

  • Commission: 18% on MRR per user.
  • Training Access: Exclusive training opportunities, including custom programs and one-on-one sessions with RouteMagic’s leadership.
  • Co-Marketing Activities:
      1. Collaborative development of joint white papers or in-depth case studies.
      2. Co-hosted events or industry conferences.
      3. First priority in all co-marketing activities, including lead generation, product launches, and joint PR efforts.
  • Requirements:
    1. Sales Volume: 200+ users onboarded.
    2. Revenue Contribution: £200,001+ in annual MRR.
    3. Customer Retention Rates: Minimum 85% annual retention rate.
    4. Training: Completion of all certification levels, with ongoing engagement in training programs.
