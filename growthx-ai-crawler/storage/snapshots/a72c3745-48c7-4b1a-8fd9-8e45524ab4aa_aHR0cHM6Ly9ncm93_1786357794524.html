Problems:

  1. Fragmented Information: Businesses cannot extract actionable insights from unstructured or missing data, preventing informed decision-making and thus ensuring a disjointed information feed.
  2. Lack of Seamless Context Sharing: Due to the disparity of the communication mediums and the teams within organizations, misinterpretation and inefficiency outcomes.
  3. Businesses experience barriers to effectively sharing and understanding context across modern channels such as social media, emails, and website interactions.
  4. Inefficient Engagement: Exhausting menu-based chat interfaces and muddled conversation systems obstruct efficient interaction with customers, which leads to poor user experience and lowered customer satisfaction.
  5. Overdependence on Human Resources: Scalability is usually hindered by human resource restraints which make it difficult for businesses to provide quality services for large numbers of customers due to lack of efficiency.
  6. Negligible Personalization: Businesses are unable to be personalized when it comes to catering to customers, and as a result, customers' needs are not satisfied, which creates a huge gap between customers and business.
  7. Complexity in Business Logic: While application logic remains a challenge to most organizations since it calls for expertise to understand and implement correctly, this affects agility and the innovation process.

​

Solution:

  1. Enhanced Insight Generation: A platform developed by Angoor AI automatizes engagement work and generates insights from customer interactions that are hyper-personalized so the business can extract data with meaning and make data-driven decisions.
  2. Seamless Context Sharing: The omnichannel CRM platform of Angoor AI provides a single source of truth across all customer touchpoints, facilitating consistent sharing of context and consistency in communication.
  3. Efficient Engagement Automation: Through the automation of engagements on multiple channels, Angoor AI systematizes customer interactions, enhancing the experience and comfort of the customers.
  4. Scalability with Reduced Dependence on Human Resources: The scalable architecture of the Angoor AI system allows companies to deal with the ever-growing flow of customer inquiries and interactions without having to depend on human resources.
  5. Hyper-personalization for Improved Customer Experience: Our platform provides personalized customer insights that are based on customer interactions. This means that a business can create targeted advertising and engagement strategies that fit individual preferences.
  6. Simplified Business Logic: Angoor AI allows the simplification of business logic into easy-to-understand terms for the stakeholders, thereby enabling them to make insightful decisions and boost business growth.

USP:

  1. Deep integration of Generative AI in features and workflows: What makes Angoor AI unique is its native Generative AI technology stack that automates conversations, provides hyper-personalized insights, and facilitates conversational analysis, thus differentiating it from conventional CRM systems.
  2. Omni-channel Integration: Similarly, Angoor AI incorporates the possibility of connecting with customers across various platforms such as social media, emails, and direct messages, ensuring a structured and consistent communication strategy.
  3. Instagram Integration: Angoor AI innovates in Instagram as a sales channel, enabling the business to expand reach and present customer engagement in the dynamic social media environment.
  4. Hyper-personalized Insights: Angoor AI is beyond the basic analytics; it uses customer interactions to provide hyper-personalized insights that allow businesses to tailor marketing campaigns and engagement strategies effectively.
  5. Simplified Business Logic: Our Flow Management System enables businesses to explain their logic in simple written English, where our proprietary Angoor Engine takes over to accomplish these tasks with our state-of-the-art context management.
  6. Scalable Architecture: Our system can handle increasing numbers of interactions without sacrificing quality or efficiency, supporting business growth and expansion plans.

To calculate the Total Addressable Market (TAM), Serviceable Available Market (SAM), and Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM) for Angoor AI, let's define these components:

1. Total Number of Potential Customers (TPC): This refers to the total number of businesses globally that could potentially use a CRM for managing customer interactions on Meta platforms. For this calculation, let's assume a rough estimate based on the number of businesses actively advertising or engaged in commerce on these platforms.

2. Average Revenue Per Customer (ARPU): This is the average revenue Angoor AI expects to generate from each customer. Given the B2B nature of the product and the outlined prices, we can estimate an ARPU.

3. Target Market Segment (TMS): This is the percentage of the total market that Angoor AI aims to capture initially. It could be defined by the focus on businesses of a certain size or within a specific geographic location.

4. Market Penetration/Share (MPS): This is the percentage of the SAM that Angoor AI realistically plans to capture within a specified time frame.

Some assumptions for these calculations:


For ICP-1:

TAM = ~$135B (CRM+Marketing industry)
- TMS: 0.1% of the global market, focusing on mid-market businesses.
- MPS: 0.1% within the first few years of operation.


Calculations:

  1. SAM (Serviceable Available Market)
    SAM = TAM * TMS
    SAM = $135,000,000,000 * 0.1% = $135,000,000
  2. SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)
    SOM = SAM * MPS
    SOM = $135,000,000 * 0.1% = $135,000


For ICP-2:

TAM = ~$1.3T (Service Industry)
- TMS: 0.05% of the global market, focusing on mid-market businesses.
- MPS: 0.05% within the first few years of operation.


Calculations:


1. SAM (Serviceable Available Market)
SAM = TAM * TMS
SAM = $1,300,000,000,000 * 0.05% = $650,000,000

2. SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)
SOM = SAM * MPS
SOM = $650,000,000 * 0.05% = $325,000

Referral Program/Partner Program

WOM is the major source of this acquisition. The referral program is just an add-on to encourage businesses to provide other customers for our platform.​

Objective:

To leverage word-of-mouth to acquire new users by rewarding existing customers for referring Angoor AI to potential clients.


Target Audience:

Existing users who are satisfied with Angoor AI and likely to recommend it, including CXOs, Directors, and Team Leads in Sales, Customer Support, and Marketing.


Referral Reward:

Both the referrer and the referee receive benefits. The referrer gets a 10% discount on their next subscription renewal for each successful referral. The referee gets a 20% discount on their first subscription.


Referral Flow:

1. User Dashboard: A dedicated section in the user dashboard for referrals where users can see their referral link and track rewards.

2. Referral Link Sharing: Easy sharing options for the referral link via email, social media, or direct message.

3. Notification: Automatic notifications for referrers when their referrals join Angoor AI, and for referees when they are eligible for discounts.


Referral Message:

"Join me in transforming customer interactions with Angoor AI! Sign up with my link and get 20% off your first subscription. I'll get a 10% discount too, so it's a win-win!"


Tracking Referrals:

A robust tracking system in the user dashboard allows users to see how many people have signed up using their link, their pending rewards, and the total discounts earned.


Strategies to Increase Referrals:

- Group Referral Incentives: Additional discounts for users who refer multiple new customers within a certain timeframe.

- Limited-Time Offers: Special promotional periods where referral rewards are temporarily increased to encourage more sharing.

- Referral Competitions: Competitions among users to see who can refer the most new customers within a period, with significant prizes for the top referrers.
