Lets understand the product now

MAZAFATI DATES i.e KIMIA DATES

Mazafati dates are a prized cultivar of date palms originating from Iran. Renowned for their luxurious qualities, they are often referred to as the “King of Dates” or the “Black Diamond of Dates” due to their:

  • Deep, almost black color: This distinct appearance distinguishes them from other date varieties.
  • Incredibly soft texture: Mazafati dates have a melt-in-your-mouth texture, making them a delightful indulgence.
  • Intense sweetness: They offer a rich and concentrated sweetness that is a true pleasure for the taste buds.
  • High moisture content: Compared to other dates, Mazafati dates have a higher moisture content, contributing to their soft and almost creamy texture.

These characteristics combine to create a truly unique and unforgettable date experience. Mazafati dates are typically enjoyed independently as a decadent treat, but their versatility allows them to be incorporated into various recipes, adding sweetness and a unique texture.

​

Mazafati dates are sometimes called Kimia dates, particularly in countries outside of Iran. Mazafati date – This is the most widely recognized name for this date variety within Iran and by date connoisseurs globally. Kimia date – This name is used in some countries, particularly in India. It’s not as common as Mazafati but can be encountered occasionally.
