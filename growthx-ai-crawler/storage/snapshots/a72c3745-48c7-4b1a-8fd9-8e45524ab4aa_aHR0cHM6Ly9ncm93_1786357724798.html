Core Value Proposition: What are we really in the business of?

Toffee Doodle is India's 1st Ready-to-Eat Pudding brand. We shine in various sweet spots:

  1. Packaged Indian Sweets: Think Rasgulla, Sonpapdi, straight from the shelf.
  2. Chocolates: A post-meal delight or a snack for those sweet cravings.
  3. Ice Cream: The perfect dessert to satisfy cravings anytime.

​

Our goal? To be your go-to sweet, savory, and gourmet snack for every occasion.

​

What problem does our product solve?

​

Industry Challenges ❌:

  • Additives: Many puddings have additives like BHA, Yellow 5, and Yellow 6, which can be harmful.
  • Unhealthy Fats: Instant puddings often contain trans and saturated fats, risking heart health.
  • Safety Risks: Spoiled mixes can have harmful bacteria, posing food-borne illness risks.
  • Cost and Competition: Fluctuating sugar and chocolate prices and fierce competition challenge profitability.

​

​What Toffee Doodle Offers ✅:

  • Quality Ingredients: Additive-free for a safe, wholesome treat.
  • Safety Measures: Rigorous safety protocols ensure every pudding is contaminant-free.
  • Convenience and Taste: Ready-to-eat, delicious puddings that don’t compromise on quality or health.


Customer Benefits 🌟

  • Pure Pleasure: Indulge in rich, creamy puddings with no unhealthy fats. Every spoonful is pure bliss.
  • Peace of Mind: Our stringent safety measures ensure worry-free indulgence.
  • Convenient Delight: Dive into deliciousness anytime, anywhere—perfect for busy lives.
  • Wholesome Enjoyment: Guilt-free treats crafted with your health in mind. Perfect for solo indulgence or sharing.

Job to Be Done

As Toffee Doodle is a new product in an existing category, our market research revealed several gaps left by competitors: ⬇


Health-Conscious Ingredients​

Issue: Competitors often use artificial additives, preservatives, and high sugar.

Opportunity: There's a gap for a healthier, all-natural pudding option.

​Shelf-Stability

​Issue: Many puddings require refrigeration.

Opportunity: A shelf-stable product would be more convenient.

Consistent Premium Quality​

Issue: Competitors lack stringent quality controls.

Opportunity: Room for products with guaranteed premium ingredients and consistent quality

Vegetarian and Dietary Preferences​

Issue: Many puddings contain gelatin and eggs.

Opportunity: Market for 100% vegetarian, egg-free, and gelatin-free options

Flavor Innovation​

Issue: Market saturated with traditional flavors.

Opportunity: Demand for innovative and international flavor profiles.

What We Will Be Optimizing:


​Consumer Trust and Safety

​Action: Ensure production processes meet the highest safety standards.

Benefit: Provides peace of mind for consumers regarding product safety and hygiene.

​Flavor Variety and Innovation

​Action: Offer a range of international and unique flavors.

Benefit: Cater to diverse tastes and preferences, unlike competitors with limited options.

Convenience and Accessibility​

​Action: Produce shelf-stable puddings.

Benefit: No need for refrigeration, making our products more accessible and convenient.

Health-Conscious Choices​

Action: Focus on natural ingredients without harmful additives.

Benefit: Positions Toffee Doodle as the go-to choice for health-conscious consumers.

Major Jobs to Be Done:

    • Explanation: Set Toffee Doodle apart with unique flavors, health-conscious ingredients, and convenience.
  3. Trust and Preference:
    • Explanation: Build consumer trust through stringent safety standards and consistent premium quality, ensuring preference over competitors.

For health-conscious dessert lovers who seek a delicious and convenient treat without compromising on quality and health, our Toffee Doodle puddings are ready-to-eat desserts that offer natural ingredients, shelf-stability, premium quality, and innovative flavors in sustainable packaging.

Finding the Brand Wedge

Toffee Doodle's brand wedge emerges from a landscape craving change. 🌟 In a world where traditional sweets often lack variety and compromise quality, our mission is clear: deliver diverse, high-quality, and consistent dessert experiences. We recognize the growing demand for healthier indulgences, the need for convenience in busy lifestyles, and the desire for desserts that promise reliability and excitement. 💫

​

Navigating through challenges, Toffee Doodle pioneers accessible indulgence. Despite the cost hurdles of healthier ingredients, we make wholesome treats affordable. We challenge the status quo by reimagining recipes, introducing fresh ideas, and proving that healthy doesn't mean sacrificing taste. Toffee Doodle is not just a brand; it's a movement—spreading awareness and empowering consumers to make informed, health-conscious choices. 🚀

​

Dreaming big, Toffee Doodle envisions a world where dessert time is synonymous with joy and satisfaction. With each pudding, we promise an escape into bliss—a moment where flavor reigns supreme, guilt is nonexistent, and convenience is king. Toffee Doodle isn't just redefining dessert; it's crafting a future where indulgence knows no compromise. 🌈


The WEDGE: "If the category perception is that traditional sweets lack variety and compromise on quality, and my user feels bored with limited choices and concerned about inconsistent quality, then my brand, Toffee Doodle, will always solve for delivering diverse, high-quality, and consistent dessert experiences. With our ready-to-eat and irresistibly tasty pudding range, we ensure that every spoonful brings joy and satisfaction, fulfilling our user's non-negotiable need for indulgence without compromise."

Avoiding Mis-Selling 🚫

  1. Crystal Clear Descriptions:
    • Honest Details: Give detailed info on each product, including ingredients, nutritional facts, and dietary info (like vegetarian, gelatin-free).
    • Health Highlights: Emphasize the health benefits, like no artificial colors or preservatives.
  3. Educate and Inform:
    • Engaging Content: Use blogs, FAQs, and social media to share the benefits of our ingredients and what makes our puddings special.
    • Knowledgeable Staff: Train customer service reps to be pudding experts who can answer any question.
  5. Ethical Marketing:
    • Straightforward Ads: Keep our advertising truthful without exaggerating health benefits.
    • Consistent Messaging: Ensure all marketing materials reflect what we genuinely offer.
  7. Feedback is Gold:
    • Encourage Reviews: Ask for feedback to understand how we’re doing and where we can improve.
    • Prompt Responses: Quickly address any customer concerns and offer solutions like refunds or replacements.
  9. Top-Notch Quality:
    • Strict Quality Checks: Maintain high standards to ensure our puddings are always top quality.
    • Open Book: Be transparent about our production process and quality checks, sharing behind-the-scenes looks or certifications.
