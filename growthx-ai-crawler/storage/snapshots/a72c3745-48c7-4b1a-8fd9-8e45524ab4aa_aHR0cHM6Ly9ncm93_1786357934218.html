Market Overview:

The trading education market sits at the intersection of financial markets participation and online skill-based learning, both of which are witnessing strong growth in India.


Key Trends and Tailwinds:

  • 📈 Rising Retail Participation - Increasing number of demat accounts and retail investors entering the stock market
  • 📱 Digital Adoption - Easy access via mobile apps (Zerodha, Groww) has lowered entry barriers
  • 🎥 Content-Led Awareness - YouTube, Instagram, and finfluencers are driving curiosity around trading
  • 💸 Side Income Mindset - Growing demand for additional income streams beyond primary jobs


Headwinds & Challenges

  • ⚠️ Low Trust in Industry - Presence of fake gurus and tip-based culture reduces credibility
  • 📉 High Failure Rate in Trading - Majority of users lose money → leads to skepticism
  • 🧠 Expectation Mismatch - Users expect quick profits vs reality of skill-building
  • 🆓 Free Content Competition - Abundance of free content (YouTube, Zerodha Varsity)
  • 📊 Regulatory Scrutiny (SEBI) - Increasing regulations on finfluencers and trading education

Total Addressable Market (TAM): This is the total revenue opportunity if BTS captured 100% of the trading education market.

It is based on:
→ Users (online + offline trading education in India)
→ Average revenue per user (ARPU) across all course segments

TAM = Users × ARPU
= 12.5M × ₹20,000
= ₹2.5 lakh crore (~$30B)


Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM): This is the segment of users who are willing to pay for structured trading education.

It is based on:
→ % of users willing to pay (~12% of serious learners)

SAM = TAM × % of serviceable market
= ₹2.5 lakh crore × 12%
= ₹0.3 lakh crore (~$3.6B)


Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM): This is the realistic market BTS can capture in the near term.

It is based on:
→ % of premium, high-intent users BTS can target (~10% of SAM)

SOM = SAM × % of obtainable market
= ₹0.3 lakh crore × 10%
= ₹0.03 lakh crore (~$360M)
