Some housekeeping

I'll be using some concepts and words that might be new to you. You might read "enterprises" a lot and wonder what an enterprise really is or what the heck "ops" is. So, let's start by defining some of this beforehand. Of course, feel free to skip this section and return later if you spot a conceptual gap.

  • Enterprise: In B2B, especially SaaS, enterprises are large companies with thousands or hundreds of thousands of employees. The definition changes for each seller. For Mindflow, any company with 5000+ employees is an enterprise. Focusing on enterprises means larger deal sizes, longer sales cycles, and more complex decision-making processes.
  • Ops: Operations. Please note that sometimes operations could be used in a software/tool context. Here, it means an API call.
  • SecOps: Any operation or work done in a cybersecurity team such as threat intelligence, phishing, endpoint protection etc.
    • Job titles: Security Analyst, SOC Office, Chief Information Security Office (CISO)
    • Examples of tools: CrowdStrike, SentinelOne, Splunk
  • CloudOps: Any operation or work done in a cloud team, such as cloud cost optimization on AWS or GCP, cloud resource management, cloud-based data security and management, etc.
    • Job titles: Cloud Engineer, DevSecOps Engineer, CloudOps Leader
    • Examples of tools: AWS (300+ services), Google Cloud Platform, or GCP (200+ services)
  • ITOps: The operations and tasks that keep your company online. This includes managing secure access for thousands of employees to company tools and resources (IAM), onboarding new people and offboarding outgoing ones, and ensuring all devices (or endpoints) are running smoothly.
    • Job titles: IT Manager, ITOps Analyst, CISO
    • Examples of tools: Jamf, Drata, Okta, 1Password
  • Services: Almost every software used by enterprises has an API. But 1 product ≠ 1 API. Sometimes, a product can have multiple APIs, each of which is also called a service. We'll use it interchangeably.

Definition & Characteristics

  • ENT are large enterprises (ex., Auchan, LVMH, Thales) with complex security environments, very-high security alert volumes, and mature security & IT teams —they know their sh*t.
  • They would 10s of thousands of employees using 100s of software tools

  • Level 2: SOC Director, Head of IT, Head of Cloud

Key Value Proposition

  • A centralized command center that connects all tools removes siloes, and increases collaboration without burdening the infra
  • 4,000+ integrations (8x closest competitor) in Mindflow —ENTs can automate 90% or more of their workflows from day 1

  • Always compliant with even the most stringent frameworks like SOC2, ISO27001, AWS Qualified, GDPR, and more

🔗 Partner selection

Security

Security is a primary use case for Mindflow. Enterprise security teams use 63 tools on average, and each of these tools comes with a degree of freedom. Unsurprisingly, security tools are not designed to connect seamlessly with other tools. Mindflow provides that integration layer.

Products: CrowdStrike, F5, Fortinet, SentinelOne, IBM QRadar, VirusTotal, Splunk, HashiCorp

IT

IT teams manage critical workflows that keep the entire company online, apps functional, and environments adequately managed. Some of these workflows can include onboarding a new team member with the right tools, setting up new products, and ensuring everyone in the company can access all the technical resources they need. Their jobs become difficult as new tools are added and in larger organizations shadow IT becomes a huge problem.

Shadow IT refers to the practice of employees using tools without proper acknowledgment and approval from security or IT teams. This puts the entire company at risk from third-party actors.

Products: Jira, Twilio, Zendesk, Jamf, Microsoft Graph, Drata, Vanta, Cloudflare, 1Password, Bitwarden

Cloud

CloudOps and DevOps teams work closely and often ensure the company products, website, and infrastructure stay online. This requires them to manage many highly technical and complex tools. A slight misstep could mean that the company's servers are down for 2 hours, which could result in millions of dollars of losses in Mindflow's market segment.

Products: AWS, GCP, Azure, Tencent Cloud, OVH, Scaleway,

Business

Mindflow has seen an organic expansion of use cases growing from security to IT, HR, and marketing teams. Being a versatile workflow automation platform, Mindflow must provide teams access to all the necessary integrations to help them automate critical, time-saving workflows.

Products: Notion, HubSpot, Salesforce, Brevo, Lemlist, OpenAI, Anthropic
