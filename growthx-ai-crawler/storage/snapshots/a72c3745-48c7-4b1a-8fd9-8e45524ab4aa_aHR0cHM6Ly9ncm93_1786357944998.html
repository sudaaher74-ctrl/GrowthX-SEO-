Frequently Asked Questions

An ICP is a detailed description of the type of customer who would benefit most from your product and is most likely to become a loyal user. Defining your ICP allows you to focus your marketing, product development, and sales strategies on customers who are the best fit, helping you increase conversions, improve retention, and drive growth efficiently.

This guide takes you step-by-step through the ICP creation process, from understanding your target audience's demographics and behaviors to mapping out their needs and pain points. It also includes strategies to find and connect with your ICP through the right channels, ensuring that you’re focused on high-impact efforts that drive real results.

No special tools are required to use the guide. However, if you have access to analytics tools (like Google Analytics, social media insights, or CRM data), these can provide additional data to help identify and refine your ICP. The guide also includes recommendations on data sources you may already have access to.

Yes, the guide is designed to help you identify and prioritize multiple ICPs if your product serves different customer segments. It includes an ICP prioritization framework that helps you decide which segments to focus on based on factors like adoption rate, lifetime value, and spending potential.

It’s a good idea to revisit your ICP regularly, especially as your business grows or as new customer insights come in. We recommend updating your ICP at least once a year or whenever you notice shifts in customer needs, behaviors, or industry trends. This guide also includes tips for refining your ICP over time, helping you stay aligned with your target audience as they evolve.
