Bubble.io


What is Bubble.io?

Bubble.io is a no-code platform that enables users to build web applications without writing any code. It provides a visual interface with drag-and-drop functionality for designing, developing, and deploying fully-functional web apps. The platform is ideal for both users with technical knowledge as well as non-developers. With its easy-to-use interface and powerful integrations, it is the perfect platform for launching a MVP in under a month & scaling the web app for a larger audience.


Features of Bubble.io

Bubble.io is one of the most powerful no-code platforms with over 2.5 million users. Some of the major features of bubble.io are:

  1. Visual Development
    It allows users to build complex applications using visual interface, making building apps easy.
  2. Database Management
    Bubble.io has built in support for designing & managing database, allowing front-end and backend within the same framework.
  3. Hosting
    Bubble takes care of hosting and makes the deploying one-click task.
  4. Workflows
    Bubble provides creation of complex app logic using workflows
  5. Integration
    Bubble is easy to integrate with any external apps or tools to expand app functionality.
  6. Templates & Plugins
    Bubble has a rich marketplace with ready-to-use templates for a easy start and helpful plugins by the community for easy integration


Core Value Proposition

Bubble.io empowers anyone to create web apps with minimal technical expertise, significantly reducing time-to-market and development costs. It also bridges the gap between ideation and execution, democratizing app development for entrepreneurs, businesses, and creators.

Monetization litmus test


Retention Graph

The industry has minimal data for retention. However, based on the user research, I was able to see a few patterns.

  1. Steep learning Curve
    Bubble has lots of resources, however, due to the platform's huge range of features, it sometimes has a steep learning curve. Users who stuck till D30 and engaged with the community were more likely to continue on Bubble for a longer period.
  2. First App Launch
    A lot of users who came to the platform, tried to build something. They either were successful or they could not create the first app. Out of the user that created & successfully launched first app remanied for at least D60, after which there was a decline due to less engagement on the app.
  3. Community Engagement
    The users who engaged with the community through forum were more likely to retain as they felt supported and loved the large & helpful community

Based on this, the retention curve tends to decline within the first 7 days, plateaus at D30, falls a bit around D60 and plateaus.
