Experiment 1: Freemium Model with Milestones for Free-to-Paid Conversion

Objective: Convert free users into paid Circle Members by showcasing the value of premium features.

Implementation Steps:

  1. Freemium Offer:
    • Provide free access to basic features like medicine delivery tracking, basic consultations, and health tips.
    • Lock premium features such as free delivery, priority consultations, discounts on diagnostics, and chronic care plans behind a membership paywall.
  3. Journey with Milestones:
    • Set milestones for users (e.g., after 3 purchases or 2 consultations) to prompt them to explore premium features.
    • Use in-app notifications like: "Unlock free delivery and save ₹300 this month with Circle Membership!"
  5. Limited-Time Offers:
    • Offer a 7-day free trial of Circle Membership to showcase benefits.
    • Post-trial, send email campaigns highlighting savings and exclusive benefits.
  7. Targeted Campaigns:
    • Use AI to identify frequent users (Core and Power Users) who are more likely to convert.
    • Send personalized emails with subject lines like: "Priya, save ₹500 on your next order with Circle Membership!"

Success Metrics:

  • Conversion rate from free to paid membership.
  • Retention rate of new paid members after the trial period.


Experiment 2: Cross-Selling Through Bundles

Objective: Increase AOV by bundling complementary products based on user behavior.

Implementation Steps:

  1. AI-Driven Recommendations:
    • Use AI to analyze purchase patterns and suggest bundles. For example:

    • Offer discounts on bundled products (e.g., "Buy sunscreen + serum together and save 15%!").
  5. In-App Prompts:
    • Display bundles during checkout with messages like: "Customers who bought this also purchased..."
  7. Personalized Email Campaigns:
    • Highlight relevant bundles based on past purchases (e.g., "Complete your skincare routine with our exclusive bundle!").

Success Metrics:

  • Increase in AOV per transaction.
  • Click-through rate (CTR) on bundle recommendations.


Experiment 3: Up-Selling Chronic Care Plans

Objective: Encourage Power Users to upgrade from recurring medicine orders to comprehensive chronic care plans.

1. Time-Based Pricing

What to Charge For:

  • Subscription plans like Circle Membership (monthly/yearly) that offer free delivery, discounts on medicines, and priority consultations.

2. Output Based Pricing

What to Charge For:

  • Pay-per-service model for consultations, diagnostics, or medicine orders.

Why It Works:

  • Users pay only for the services they consume, similar to Urban Company’s approach.

3. Access Based Pricing

What to Charge For:

  • Access to Apollo’s healthcare ecosystem (e.g., chronic care plans or EMR tracking).
  • Example: ₹400/year for exclusive chronic care benefits like reminders and free delivery.

Why It Works:

  • Users pay for the right to access premium features regardless of usage frequency, similar to AWS’s pricing model.

4. Shareability-Based Pricing

What to Charge For:

  • Family plans where multiple members can share a single subscription.
  • Example: ₹500/month for a family plan covering up to 4 members.

Why It Works:

  • Appeals to families managing healthcare collectively, increasing perceived value

    • The original price is shown as ₹399 and ₹599 but is slashed to ₹149 and ₹199, making the discounted price seem like a great deal.
    • This leverages the anchoring bias, where users perceive the discounted price as more valuable compared to the original price.
  3. Scarcity Bias:
    • The mention of "Save every time you order medicines" and exclusive benefits (e.g., free health tests) creates a sense of urgency and exclusivity, encouraging users to act quickly.
  5. Social Proof:
    • The #CircleFact ("On average, Circle members save up to ₹400 every month") provides evidence of value, appealing to users' trust in others’ experiences.
  7. Simplified Choices:
    • Only two plans are offered (6 months and 12 months), reducing decision fatigue and leveraging the paradox of choice (fewer options make decisions easier).
Cons of Current Pricing Page
  1. Lack of Default Option (Decoy Effect Missing):
    • There’s no clear “default” plan highlighted as the most popular or best value, which could guide users toward a specific choice (e.g., the 12-month plan).
    • A decoy plan (e.g., a higher-priced 3-month plan) could nudge users toward the more profitable 12-month plan.
  3. Insufficient Emphasis on Benefits:
    • While benefits are listed, they are not visually emphasized or tied directly to user savings. This misses an opportunity to leverage the loss aversion bias, where users fear missing out on significant benefits.
  5. No Urgency Triggers:
    • There’s no mention of limited-time offers or deadlines for discounts, which could activate the scarcity bias more effectively.
  7. No Personalization:
    • The page does not tailor recommendations based on user behavior (e.g., frequent buyers vs. casual users). Personalization could increase perceived value for different user segments.
Suggestions for Redesigning the Pricing Page
1. Add a Default Plan with Decoy Effect
  • Highlight the 12-month plan as the “Best Value” option with a visual badge like “Most Popular” or “Best Deal.”
  • Introduce a decoy plan (e.g., ₹99 for 3 months) priced closer to the 6-month plan to make the 12-month plan more appealing by comparison.
2. Emphasize Benefits Visually
  • Use icons and bold text to highlight key benefits for each plan:
    • Example: “Save ₹400/month,” “Free Health Test Worth ₹500,” etc.
  • Add a savings calculator showing how much users save annually with each plan.
3. Create Urgency
  • Add time-sensitive messaging like:
    • “Offer valid till February 15, 2025!”
    • “Limited-time discount—Save 50% today!”
4. Incorporate Social Proof
  • Include testimonials or stats like:
    • “Join over 1 million satisfied Circle members!”
    • “95% of members renew their subscription.”
5. Personalized Recommendations
  • Use AI-driven prompts based on user behavior:
    • Example: “As a frequent buyer, you’ll save ₹2,000/year with our 12-month plan!”
