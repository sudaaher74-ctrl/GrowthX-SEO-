🪝 Product hook:

Jupiter GoalPots

Goal

Encourage users to save for specific goals (like buying a car or any other asset) by using a dedicated, goal-oriented financial pot within Jupiter Money app.

Success Metric

Increase in the number of users setting up goal-oriented pots and achieving their saving targets.

Problem Statement

Many users struggle to save for big purchases due to lack of structured saving plans, visible progress tracking, and investing plans.

Current alternative

Users currently might use generic savings accounts without specific targets or tracking, or use other apps that might not integrate seamlessly with their primary banking.

Solution

Introduce a feature in the Jupiter app that allows users to create customized financial pots for specific goals, like purchasing a car. The app will provide tools for setting a timeline, calculating the required monthly savings, and tracking progress.

Users can set up a new pot by specifying their goal (e.g., buying a car), the total amount needed, and their timeline. The app then calculates how much they need to save monthly and allows them to set up automatic transfers to this pot. Users can track their progress through visual indicators and receive tips and reminders to stay on track.

Whimsical: https://whimsical.com/solution-producthook-8qU1eGQNdjxh2p1jtocvom

Metrics to track

  • Number of goal-oriented pots created.
  • Percentage of users reaching at least 50% of their target amount.
  • User engagement with the feature (frequency of checking pot, adjusting settings, etc.).

Ramp up milestones

  • Provides a clear and structured way for users to save for specific goals.
  • Visual progress tracking and reminders enhance user engagement and motivation.
  • Integrates goal-oriented saving within the broader financial management ecosystem of the app, making it convenient and efficient.
