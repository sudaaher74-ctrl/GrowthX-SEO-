Market Size

Current Stats:

Average Revenue Per User (ARPU):

Crypto platforms in India charge roughly 1% transaction fee overall.

The average trading volume per user is roughly $6000

Hence, ARPU = 6000 * 0.01 = $60/user

Potential Customers:

The estimated users in 2025 is 100M [data]

Market Sizing

Based on the above calculations,

TAM (Total Addressable Market):

TAM = ARPU X Total Users = 60 * 100M = $ 6B


This is quite close with Forbes data of Indian market size of 6.5 B USD.


SAM (Serviceable Addressable Market)

Based on the chosen ICPs, volume from Traders would constitute of around 70% [Mudrex internal data].

Hence,

SAM = 0.7 X SAM = 0.7 X 6 B = $ 4.2B


SOM (Serviceable Obtainable Market)

Assuming the top 5 crypto players in India capturing 80% of the market. The distribution can be assumed to be equally among these players.

Hence, SOM for Mudrex will be:

SOM = 0.2 X 0.8 X SAM = 0.16 X 4.2 = 0.672 B = $ 670 M


Hence, we come to

TAM: $6B

SAM: $4.2B

SOM: $670M


Total Users: 100 M

Content Loop - Social Portfolio

The content loop revolves around sharable trading strategies and social portfolios, using insights from successful traders. The goal is to turn professional traders into creators while giving new users access to tested strategies.

The content loop will be created in such way that experienced traders can share their strategies and portfolio insights as "Social Portfolio", attracting new and intermediate traders to engage with and adopt the platform. This loop will in turn make the customers who using other's social portfolio to create their own social portfolio and share with the next set of customers.

Breakdown

Hook

  • Social Proof: Seeing successful traders and real portfolio performance stats.
  • FOMO: Exclusive trading insights and high-performing strategies.
  • Gamification: Leaderboards and rankings among top traders.
  • Monetization Opportunity: Professional traders to be able monetize their strategies.

CAC : LTV

To estimate CAC, we will assume:

  1. Ad Costs (CPM & CPC)
    1. Twitter (X) Ads: $5 - $10 CPM, $0.50 - $2 CPC
    2. Meta (Instagram) Ads: $3 - $7 CPM, $0.30 - $1.50 CPC
  3. Click-Through Rate (CTR)
    1. Twitter: 0.5% - 1%
    2. Meta: 1% - 3%
  5. Conversion Rate
    1. General benchmark for fintech/crypto platforms: 3% - 8%
    2. Assuming 5% conversion from sign-ups to active traders


Using this data,

Twitter CAC Calculation

  • Assume $7 CPM (Cost per 1,000 impressions)
  • Assume 1% CTR → 10 clicks per 1,000 impressions
  • Assume $1.5 CPC (Cost per Click)
  • Assume 5% Sign-up to conversion


Twitter CAC = ~$30 per active trader


Meta CAC Calculation

  • Assume $5 CPM
  • Assume 2% CTR → 20 clicks per 1,000 impressions
  • Assume $1 CPC
  • Assume 5% Sign-up to conversion


Meta CAC = ~$20 per active trader


Blended CAC from both platforms will be around $25


Based on our earlier calculation during Market Sizing, LTV of a user = $60

Hence,

CAC: LTV would be 0.42 which is okay, but not great according to industry standards.
